# Originality Policy

STATUS: COMPLETE (Gate 0)

## 1. The rejected branch

`claude/last-tenant-part-one-ch1-4-nfwdvx` (this branch's sibling, not
its ancestor — this branch was created as an orphan specifically so
there is no shared git history) contains a different, rejected novel:
Nora Hale, a chartered surveyor; Cass Ferris, a missing journalist;
Julian Cole, a landlord-developer; Desmond Pye, a caretaker; the city of
Rookford; a converted mill called Sovereign Mill; a fire-safety
cover-up mystery.

**None of that material is canon for THE LAST TENANT as defined in this
repository.** This is stricter than "don't copy sentences." Specifically
excluded:

- Any character with the same function-plus-affect combination (e.g., a
  taciturn building caretaker who "says more than he means to and then
  retreats" is excluded even under a new name, because it's the same
  character doing the same job in the same way).
- The central image of a building that physically conceals the truth of
  what happened to a missing woman inside its own fabric, if executed the
  same way (a hidden construction defect, a room that's "the only
  complete thing" in an otherwise stalled renovation, etc.). Bellwether
  House's own version of "the building holds evidence" (analog
  maintenance logs, service corridors, physical rather than digital
  traces) must arise from *this* book's premise (identity engineering
  needs analog, human-witnessed continuity — see `palinode_system.md`),
  not be a reskin of the fire-safety mechanism.
- Any clue-planting pattern lifted structurally (a torn page found under
  a skirting board that matches a notebook found later; a portal/account
  left logged into a previous occupant's identity) even with surface
  details changed.
- A protagonist whose professional competence is undermined by an
  identical personal wound-shape (a spouse's hidden financial betrayal
  discovered via a letter). Mara's wound (see `03_characters/mara_voss.md`)
  must remain about verification-as-armor and the sisters' relationship,
  not be a renamed version of Nora's marriage.

If a future session notices a scene, image, or structural beat converging
on the rejected branch's material — even by coincidence, even if
independently generated — stop and change it. Coincidental convergence on
a *rejected* predecessor in the same repository is a project-specific
risk this policy exists to catch; it is treated as seriously as an
external plagiarism risk would be.

## 2. Originality with respect to the wider genre

The rejected-branch rule above is about this repository's own history.
Separately, the manuscript must also clear the ordinary bar for
published psychological thrillers: it should read as in conversation
with the genre (missing-person thrillers, identity-fraud thrillers,
unreliable-witness structures), not as a copy of any specific existing
work. No specific comparable titles are treated as source material to
imitate. `12_publishing/` may eventually position the book against real
comps for marketing purposes once it exists in finished form — that is a
positioning exercise, not a drafting instruction, and the two must not be
confused.

Genre convention is not the same as cliché. Using a missing-person
inciting incident, an investigator protagonist, or a Brooklyn apartment
building is convention — expected furniture of the genre, fine to use.
Cliché is the *execution* going flat: stock phrasing, stock character
beats, stock reveals. `01_author_bible/banned_cliches.md` governs prose-
and beat-level cliché; this policy governs structural/creative
originality.

## 3. No overclaiming

Nothing produced in this repository — not the charter, not the
publishing materials, not casual notes in any file — may assert or
imply:

- guaranteed commercial success;
- guaranteed bestseller status;
- guaranteed award recognition;
- a guaranteed or even likely adaptation deal.

"Limited-series adaptation potential" (used in `project_charter.md` and
elsewhere) describes a structural property of the material — a
contained cast, a single central location, escalating episodic beats —
and nothing more. Any future publishing-facing document
(`12_publishing/query_letter.md`, `pitch materials`, etc.) must be
checked against this rule before use.

## 4. No operational harm

This is a novel about a real-world category of crime (identity fraud,
synthetic identity fraud, staged disappearance). The manuscript may
*characterize* Palinode's methods at the level of plausible thriller
detail — named categories of technique, the shape of what's hard and
what's costly about it, the human vulnerabilities it exploits — but must
never assemble into an actual usable how-to: no real specific document
templates, no real specific system exploits, no step-by-step sequence a
reader could follow to manufacture a synthetic identity or disappear
undetected. `02_novel_bible/palinode_system.md` and
`09_research/LEGAL_RISK_NOTES.md` are the standing check for this; any
research finding that risks crossing this line gets flagged there before
it's used in any other file.

## 5. Fabrication discipline

Research claims used to ground the novel's real-world texture (insurance
investigation practice, NYPD missing-persons procedure, synthetic
identity fraud definitions, property records practice, etc.) are never
invented and presented as fact. Every such claim is either sourced (with
citation, access date, and confidence level, in
`09_research/SOURCE_LOG.md`) or explicitly logged as an open question in
`09_research/FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md`. "This is probably how it works" is not
sufficient basis for a claim the manuscript will rely on for plausibility.
