# Author Positioning

STATUS: DRAFT — Gate 1 level; refine once sample chapters exist to check
against.

## The authorial voice this book needs

Not a pen name or persona exercise — a statement of the *register* the
prose should consistently occupy, so any future drafting session
(including one with no memory of this one) writes in a recognizably
consistent voice.

This book reads like it was written by someone who trusts the reader's
intelligence and the material's inherent tension, and therefore doesn't
need to work hard to convince you something is scary or sad — it shows
you the specific thing and lets you have the reaction. The narrator is
controlled, exact, psychologically fluent without being clinical or
therapy-language about it (see `prose_style_guide.md`'s ban on
therapy-language exposition), and willing to sit in ambiguity rather than
resolve it early for comfort.

## Comparable register (craft touchstones, not comps to imitate)

Aim for prose that could sit comfortably next to well-regarded literary-
leaning psychological suspense — precise, humane, unhurried where the
scene earns it, taut where it doesn't — without naming or imitating any
specific author's actual sentences. This is a craft-register note, not
permission to pattern-match a real writer's style; see
`00_project/originality_policy.md` §2.

## What the voice is not

- Not hardboiled/noir pastiche.
- Not cozy-mystery warm.
- Not thriller-pulp breathless (no chapter should read like its only job
  is to end on a jolt).
- Not academic or overly interior in a way that stalls plot momentum —
  Mara's precision is a personality trait rendered through action and
  specific observation, not through extended abstract rumination.

## Consistency mechanism

Because this book will very likely be drafted across many sessions with
no shared memory, `prose_style_guide.md`, `dialogue_guide.md`, and
`banned_cliches.md` exist specifically to make this voice *checkable*,
not just describable. Any future session should be able to hold a
drafted page up against those three files and get a concrete pass/fail
on most voice questions, rather than relying on a vibe.
