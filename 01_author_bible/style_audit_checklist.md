# Style Audit Checklist

STATUS: DRAFT — Gate 1 level tool, to be used as the Voice/Prose Audit
step of `00_project/production_workflow.md`'s drafting workflow once
Gate 5 closes and chapters exist. Not yet applicable — no manuscript
prose exists in this repository.

Run every item against a drafted (post-revision) chapter. A "no" on any
item is a required fix, not a note for later.

## POV / narrative distance
- [ ] No sentence asserts another character's private interior state as
  narrated fact (see `narrative_distance.md`'s test).
- [ ] Julian Vale's interiority is never accessed anywhere in the
  chapter.
- [ ] Any document/transcript insert is justified by the chapter brief,
  not used as a scene-avoidance crutch.
- [ ] Any Elena fragment gives the reader something unreconstructable
  from Mara's own investigation.

## Fair play
- [ ] Nothing Mara has concluded is withheld from the reader purely for
  suspense.
- [ ] No chapter ending manufactures a cliffhanger from an artificial
  information gap between what Mara knows and what the narration admits.

## Banned language
- [ ] Full-text check against every item in `banned_cliches.md` —
  genre-wide list and project-specific rejected-branch list both.
- [ ] No therapy-language self-diagnosis in dialogue.
- [ ] No aphorism-density spike for any character.

## Sentence-level craft
- [ ] Sentence length varies within the chapter — no extended run of
  similar-length sentences.
- [ ] Filtering words (noticed/felt/saw/realized) are cut except where
  the act of noticing is the point.
- [ ] No two consecutive paragraphs open with the same subject/structure.
- [ ] Em dash frequency checked against the chapter's own established
  rhythm and against prior chapters — flag if creeping upward without
  purpose.

## Chapter-level discipline
- [ ] Opens in scene, not reflection/weather/throat-clearing.
- [ ] Closes on forward pressure, not summary.
- [ ] Answers, explicitly, "what did this chapter do for the sisters'
  relationship" — per `02_novel_bible/thematic_argument.md`'s emotional-
  core protection clause — not just "what did this chapter do for the
  mystery."

## Voice consistency
- [ ] Mara's voice matches her established speech/thought pattern in
  `03_characters/mara_voss.md` (once fully built at Gate 2).
- [ ] Every speaking character's dialogue is distinguishable from every
  other's without dialogue tags, per `dialogue_guide.md`.

## Cross-chapter (run at every combined audit, not just per-chapter)
- [ ] No distinctive phrase or image repeated across chapters closely
  enough to read as a tic rather than a documented, deliberate motif.
- [ ] Any newly-noticed cross-chapter repetition gets either documented
  as intentional (with reasoning) or varied — never left unaddressed.
