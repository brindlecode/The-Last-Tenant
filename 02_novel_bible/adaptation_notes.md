# Adaptation Notes

STATUS: DRAFT — positioning notes only. Per
`00_project/originality_policy.md` §3, nothing here asserts or implies
that adaptation is likely, in progress, or guaranteed. This file
describes structural properties of the material that make it a plausible
adaptation candidate, for future publishing-positioning use, not a
prediction or a claim.

## Structural properties relevant to episodic adaptation

- **A contained, nameable cast**: seven witnesses, each carrying one
  distinct account/relationship to the missing woman, map naturally onto
  an episodic structure (one witness's account per episode or pair of
  episodes) without requiring invention beyond what the novel already
  has.
- **A single central location**: Bellwether House functions as a
  standing set, reducing the world-building overhead adaptation usually
  requires.
- **A dual mystery with different reveal cadences**: the "what happened
  to Elena" mystery and the slower "what happened to Mara's own
  childhood" mystery can be paced somewhat independently, which suits a
  multi-episode structure's need for mid-season and season-end
  escalation points distinct from each other.
- **A two-hander emotional core** (the sisters) that can anchor
  marketing and performance in a way a pure ensemble piece can't.

## What this file is not

Not a pitch document, not a claim about interest from any buyer, not a
promise of any kind about the book's commercial trajectory. Revisit only
once a complete manuscript exists; premature adaptation-focused thinking
risks distorting novel-first structural decisions (see
`04_plot/` — chapter structure should serve the novel, with episodic
compatibility as a fortunate side effect, never the reverse).
