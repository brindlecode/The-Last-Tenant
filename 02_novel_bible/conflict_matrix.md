# Conflict Matrix

STATUS: DRAFT — Gate 1 level; full scene-by-scene conflict mapping is
Gate 4 work via `04_plot/scene_inventory.md`.

Major conflict pairs, what each side wants, and why it can't be
resolved quickly (the thing that sustains 46 chapters of friction rather
than one conversation).

| Pair | A wants | B wants | Why it can't resolve in one scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mara vs. Elena (relationship, pre- and reconstructed through the book) | Proof, consistency, the ability to trust what's verifiable | To be believed without having to prove herself first | Both wants are reasonable and mutually exclusive in the moment they were formed; resolving requires Mara to change her operating principle, not win an argument |
| Mara vs. Palinode/Julian Vale | The truth, fully, and Elena back | Continued concealment, framed by Julian as protection of everyone else Palinode shelters | Julian's position has real moral weight (see `palinode_system.md`), so this isn't a simple good/evil standoff — every confrontation has to reckon with his argument, not just defeat it |
| Mara vs. NYPD/Det. Teo Marchetti | To pursue the investigation her way, using expertise police process doesn't have | Procedural integrity, jurisdiction, a case that doesn't get compromised by an amateur (however expert) | Both are right by their own lights; Marchetti's caution is professionally correct even when it's frustrating — see `risk_register.md` R-006 |
| Mara vs. Adrienne Shaw | An honest answer about what her employer has known/suppressed | To protect her institution, her career, and (complicatedly) Mara herself, without fully explaining why | Adrienne's motives are mixed — genuine care for Mara plus genuine self-protection plus genuine institutional loyalty — which is what keeps this from resolving as a simple betrayal reveal |
| Elena vs. Palinode/Julian Vale | Proof of what happened to her family, usable against the organization | To reabsorb, neutralize, or repurpose her before she can use what she's found | Elena has genuine skill at exactly what Palinode does, which is why she got as far as she did and also why the organization can't simply dismiss her |
| The seven witnesses vs. the truth about "their" version of Elena | To keep the relationship/memory they had as meaningful as it felt | The investigation requires reclassifying that memory as (partly) engineered | Their grief/attachment is real regardless of the engineering, which is the thematic point (`thematic_argument.md`) as much as a plot conflict |
| Gabriel Knox vs. himself | To keep his job, his home (he lives in the building), and his sense of himself as a decent person | The accumulating cost of twelve years of not asking questions | Internal, not resolvable by an external confrontation alone — his arc has to be about him choosing to stop, not just getting caught |

## Standing rule

Every major conflict pair above must be checked, at Gate 4, for whether
it escalates or just repeats across the chapters it appears in. A
conflict that's in the same place in Chapter 30 as it was in Chapter 15
is a stalled subplot, not a sustained one.
