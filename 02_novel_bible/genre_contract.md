# Genre Contract

STATUS: Gate 1 COMPLETE at this level.

## Genre position

Adult psychological thriller, with domestic-suspense and mystery
elements. Not horror. Not supernatural. Not literary fiction as a primary
identity (though the prose should be good enough to read as well-made —
see `01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md`). Not cozy. Not a procedural
in the police-procedural sense (Mara is not a cop, and the book is
explicit — see `risk_register.md` R-006 — that this is not incidental).

## What readers of this genre are owed, and how this book pays it

- **A mystery that's actually solvable.** Every reader of a fair-play
  psychological thriller is implicitly promised that the truth was
  reachable before the reveal, for a reader paying close attention. This
  book's specific version of that promise: the seven accounts are not
  lies to catch, they're genuine partial truths to reconcile — the
  "solving" is pattern-recognition across real evidence, not catching
  someone in a contradiction. Enforced at Gate 3 by
  `05_mystery/fairness_audit.md`.
- **Escalating, controlled tension**, not constant melodrama. Tension
  comes from what's being withheld or misunderstood between characters
  who have real reasons to withhold or misunderstand, not from
  authorial trickery. `01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md`'s banned-
  devices list exists partly to protect this promise at the sentence
  level.
- **A protagonist worth the investment.** Mara has to be competent
  enough to trust and flawed enough to worry about — see
  `03_characters/mara_voss.md`.
- **Genuine stakes that are personal, not just procedural.** The sisters'
  relationship is the stakes engine; see `thematic_argument.md`.
- **A satisfying, non-cheap resolution.** Per `04_plot/ending_options.md`,
  the selected ending resolves the central mystery and the sisters'
  relationship without either resolution feeling like it was purchased
  cheaply (no last-minute confession from nowhere, no coincidence doing
  the story's work — checked at Gate 3/5).
- **A believable, contained antagonist force.** Palinode must never
  read as omnipotent — see `palinode_system.md`'s explicit realism
  constraints. A thriller antagonist that can do anything makes every
  scene's tension fake; this book's antagonist is powerful specifically
  because of what it *can't* do without exposure, which is where its
  vulnerabilities (and the plot's engine) live.

## Tone boundaries

- Grounded, not pulpy. Emotional intelligence over melodrama (see
  `01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md`).
- Dread and tension built from specific, concrete detail (a records
  discrepancy, a wrong date in an otherwise-perfect account, an object
  that doesn't belong) — not from generic atmosphere (fog, silence,
  unnamed dread). This is a craft rule as much as a genre one; see
  `banned_cliches.md`.
- Violence, where it occurs, is consequential and infrequent, not
  spectacle. This is a thriller about the violence of erasure and
  disbelief more than the violence of physical harm — physical danger
  should be real when it appears, not a default engine for chapter
  endings.
- Moral complexity is a genre requirement here, not decoration — see
  `thematic_argument.md`. A genre reader picking this book up should be
  able to trust that "the villain has a point, sometimes" is earned, not
  a gesture.

## What this book explicitly does not do

- Does not resolve into "everyone was lying" — the seven witnesses are
  the opposite of an unreliable-narrator gimmick; they are reliable
  narrators of incompatible realities.
- Does not use amnesia, dissociative identity, or supernatural
  possession as a mechanism for any of the seven accounts — every
  account is explained by Palinode's engineered continuity, never by a
  psychological or supernatural twist substituting for it.
- Does not resolve the "why can't police just solve this" tension by
  making the police incompetent — see `risk_register.md` R-006; the
  friction is jurisdictional and procedural, not a competence gap, and
  Detective Teo Marchetti (`03_characters/investigative_counterpart.md`)
  exists partly to keep this honest.
