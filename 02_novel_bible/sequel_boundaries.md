# Sequel Boundaries

STATUS: DRAFT — Gate 1 level policy statement.

## The rule

Per `00_project/project_charter.md`'s non-goals: **this is a standalone
novel.** It is not being written as a series opener. This has concrete
consequences for what the ending is allowed to do — see
`04_plot/ending_options.md`, where Architecture 4 ("The Inherited
System") was rejected as primary specifically because its unrefined form
reads as a series hook.

## What "standalone" requires of the ending, specifically

- Palinode's central threat to Mara and Elena must be resolved, not
  merely paused, by the end of the book.
- Mara must not end the book with ongoing operational control of or
  dependency on Palinode's infrastructure as an unresolved status quo.
- The sisters' relationship must reach a genuine resolution point (not
  necessarily total, uncomplicated reconciliation — see
  `04_plot/ending_options.md`'s Architecture 2 discussion of "resolution"
  as honest understanding versus full reconciliation — but a real
  endpoint, not a cliffhanger).
- No major mystery element (the seven witnesses' true stories, the
  childhood event, Palinode's fate) may be left deliberately dangling
  for a hypothetical future book.

## What's allowed

Thematic openness (the identity questions the book raises don't need
tidy universal answers) is fine and intended — that's different from
plot openness. A reader should be able to imagine these characters'
lives continuing without needing to; the book should not require a
sequel to feel finished.

## If a sequel is ever considered later

That would be a deliberate, separate, future decision — not something
this repository's planning documents should hedge toward by leaving
plot threads conveniently loose. If it happens, it happens as a new
decision recorded in `DECISION_LOG.md` at that time, not as something
baked in now.
