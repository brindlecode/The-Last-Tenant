# Stakes Matrix

STATUS: DRAFT — Gate 1 level (sufficient to validate the concept carries
real stakes throughout); full per-chapter stakes tracking is Gate 4 work
via `04_plot/tension_curve.md` and `emotional_curve.md`.

Per major thread, stakes are listed at four levels — personal, moral,
professional/institutional, physical — since a thriller that only
escalates one of these at a time tends to flatten. A chapter that raises
only physical stakes while every other level goes quiet is a warning
sign worth checking against this file at Gate 4.

## Mara Voss

- **Personal**: her only sibling; the last chance to undo a lifetime
  pattern of choosing proof over belief.
- **Moral**: whether she can act on trust without evidence, even once;
  what she's willing to become (an identity-builder herself) to protect
  someone.
- **Professional/institutional**: her career, her credibility at her
  employer, potential legal exposure for how she conducts an
  investigation involving her own family.
- **Physical**: real but not constant — Palinode's realism constraints
  (`palinode_system.md`) mean danger to Mara should be earned by specific
  operational risk (she's gotten close to something specific), not
  ambient thriller atmosphere.

## Elena Voss

- **Personal**: whether she can be known and loved as herself rather
  than as any of the identities she's worn.
- **Moral**: her own complicity in Palinode's harms as an adult
  operative — she is not purely a victim in this story.
- **Professional/institutional**: none in the conventional sense; her
  "career" is the identity work itself, which is the moral stake above
  restated.
- **Physical**: real and immediate for most of the book (she is, after
  all, missing, and later in danger as a direct consequence of what she
  found).

## Julian Vale / Palinode

- **Personal**: the meaning of a life's work he still believes was
  necessary.
- **Moral**: whether the organization's founding altruism still
  justifies what it has become — his internal answer is yes; the book's
  answer is more complicated.
- **Professional/institutional**: decades of operational security,
  dozens of protected identities whose safety depends on Palinode's
  continued concealment — this is Palinode's strongest claim to
  sympathy and must be kept visible even at the climax.
- **Physical**: his own exposure/downfall risk.

## Adrienne Shaw

- **Personal**: her mentorship of Mara, complicated by what she's known
  and not disclosed.
- **Moral**: institutional loyalty versus the specific person she
  recruited and shaped.
- **Professional/institutional**: her career and the insurance company's
  own exposure if her compromised history becomes public.
- **Physical**: low, unless Gate 3/4 finds a reason to raise it.

## The seven witnesses, collectively

- **Personal**: each has a real relationship with "Elena" (under
  whatever name) that turns out to have been partly manufactured — the
  stake is whether that relationship's *meaning* survives learning its
  circumstances were engineered.
- **Moral**: complicity by varying degrees (Gabriel Knox especially) and
  the cost of having their testimony treated as unreliable once the
  pattern emerges.
- **Physical**: primarily Evelyn Price's storyline ("Lena Hart") if
  exposure risks her/the identity she sheltered — see
  `risk_register.md` R-016 and `thematic_argument.md`'s "violence of
  forcing someone back" theme.
