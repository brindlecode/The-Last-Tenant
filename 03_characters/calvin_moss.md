# Calvin Moss

STATUS: Gate 2 COMPLETE. Stereotype-risk and consistency review recorded
in `00_project/gate_2_character_validation.md`. **Standing instruction
from the source brief, non-negotiable**: his agoraphobia/mobility
limitation is handled without stereotype — not pathologized, not
infantilized, not played for pity or comic relief. He is competent and
self-directed; his condition is a fact of his life, not the narrative's
judgment of him.

- **Full name**: Calvin Moss.
- **Age**: 47.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as a multilingual records
  specialist working under another name.
- **Occupation**: Data broker / records researcher, former skip tracer.
  Freelance, entirely remote-capable — a real, legal, regulated
  profession (`09_research/SOURCE_LOG.md` Entry 6), not an invented
  gray-market one.
- **Condition**: Agoraphobia with associated panic symptoms, onset in
  his early thirties following a specific incident (a severe panic
  attack on a crowded subway platform that he came to associate,
  irrationally but powerfully, with the platform itself, and then
  gradually with unfamiliar public spaces generally) — a specific,
  clinically plausible origin rather than an unexplained trait, built
  from general research this session (see Research needs) while
  respecting that a genuine first-person-informed read remains required
  before any scene drafts his interiority in depth. He has built a
  fully functioning professional and personal life around working from
  home over the fifteen years since; this reads as competence and
  adaptation, not tragedy.
- **Physical presence**: Rarely leaves his apartment; his home is his
  entire operational base, organized with real intent (a workspace, not
  clutter-as-metaphor for his condition).
- **Family and cultural background**: LOCKED, Gate 2 — grew up in
  Staten Island; parents both deceased (father, when Calvin was 26;
  mother, three years before story present). One younger sister,
  living in New Jersey, relationship affectionate but attenuated by his
  condition — she has, over the years, stopped expecting him at family
  gatherings, which he experiences with genuine ambivalence (relief and
  loss both real, neither canceling the other). This working
  estrangement-by-distance, not conflict, is the specific reason his
  professional relationship with "her" (Elena, under her records-
  specialist identity) became more significant to him than it might
  read on its surface — she was one of very few people who engaged with
  him as a full equal in recent years.
- **Education**: Largely self-taught in data/records work, with some
  formal community-college coursework in library science completed
  entirely online after his agoraphobia onset — a specific, plausible
  detail showing adaptation rather than limitation.
- **Economic situation**: Makes a real, if unglamorous, living from
  freelance data-broker work, increasingly affected by tightening
  industry regulation (`SOURCE_LOG.md` Entry 6) — a grounded, current,
  non-magical source of professional pressure independent of anything
  Mara or Elena does.
- **Living conditions**: Apartment doubles as an operations center —
  multiple monitors, a genuinely organized system, not "organized
  chaos" played for eccentricity.
- **Habits**: Has adapted his entire working and social life to be
  fully remote-capable, competently, over time — video calls, phone,
  digital collaboration used confidently, not as a workaround he
  resents.
- **Speech pattern**: Precise, technical, slightly formal in register
  from limited day-to-day in-person social practice — not written as
  awkward-for-comedy.
- **Private vocabulary**: Data-broker and records-research jargon.
- **Contradictions**: Knows more than almost anyone about how to find
  people through data trails — and has built his own life to be
  deliberately hard to find or reach in person.
- **Desire**: Maintain his controlled, safe, functional environment;
  solve the intellectual puzzle of who "she" really was, which genuinely
  interests him professionally.
- **Need**: To let someone into his physical space, literally or
  figuratively, for the first time in a long time — a small, real,
  specific piece of growth, not a "cure" arc.
- **Fear**: Being forced into a physical or social situation beyond his
  own control.
- **Shame**: May feel, in unguarded moments, that his condition makes
  him "less" in ways the narrative itself must not endorse or frame as
  true.
- **Secret**: LOCKED, Gate 3 — his past freelance work included building
  parts of the shell-entity/property-paper-trail infrastructure
  connected to Apartment 4C's ownership, for a client he was never told
  the ultimate purpose of and had no reason to suspect at the time
  (`05_mystery/evidence_provenance.md`) — a moral gray zone parallel to
  Jonah Bell's, in a different register: Calvin's not-knowing is
  genuine, not willful, unlike Jonah's knowing complicity.
- **False belief**: That staying fully contained and controlled keeps
  him safe from everything, including connection.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Conducted almost entirely remotely (phone,
  text, video) for most of the book — itself a useful, distinct
  texture among the seven witness relationships, none of which should
  play out identically.
- **Relationship to Elena**: Intellectual and professional respect,
  established later in Elena's fourteen-to-eighteen-month Bellwether
  House campaign as her evidence-gathering intensified and she needed
  his specific technical skill (`05_mystery/elena_timeline.md`) — she
  was one of very few people who engaged with him as a full equal.
- **Beginning knowledge**: Holds technical/data evidence relevant to the
  investigation that he hasn't had a reason to examine as evidence
  before.
- **Ending knowledge**: LOCKED — realizes, with Mara's prompting, that
  specific past technical work he did maps directly onto Bellwether
  House's own ownership structure — a genuine collaborative "aha,"
  paralleling Rina Cho's arc in structure but distinct in content and
  register.
- **Behavioral tells**: TBD, Gate 4 — deferred deliberately: a first-
  person/lived-experience research read (still required, see below)

- **Arc**: Isolated but fully competent → opens up in a small, real way
  → contributes crucial technical evidence on his own terms.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: The character positioned to
  credibly explain and demonstrate data-broker-level vulnerabilities —
