# Calvin Moss

STATUS: Gate 2 COMPLETE, AMENDED post-red-team (see
`00_project/gate_3_mystery_validation.md` amendment 12 and
`00_project/red_team_review.md`). Two structural findings, independently
corroborated, were addressed: (1) his professional usefulness to the
mystery was causally derived from his agoraphobia's onset in the prior
version — the classic instrumentalized-disability pattern, now fixed by
giving him a professional history that predates the condition; (2)
"agoraphobia/mobility limitation" wrongly conflated an anxiety disorder
with a physical disability — now separated throughout. **Standing
instruction, unchanged**: his agoraphobia is handled without stereotype
— not pathologized, not infantilized, not played for pity or comic
relief. He is competent and self-directed; his condition is a fact of
his life, not the narrative's judgment of him.

- **Full name**: Calvin Moss.
- **Age**: 47.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as a multilingual records
  specialist working under another name.
- **Occupation**: Data broker / records researcher, former skip tracer.
  Freelance, entirely remote-capable — a real, legal, regulated
  profession (`09_research/SOURCE_LOG.md` Entry 6), not an invented
  gray-market one. **Career history now decoupled from his condition
  (see below)**: he entered records/data research work in his early
  twenties, years before his agoraphobia's onset — an independent
  aptitude and career choice, not a consequence of the condition.
- **Condition**: Agoraphobia with associated panic symptoms — an anxiety
  disorder, not a physical or mobility disability; the two are no longer
  paired or treated as interchangeable anywhere in this file or this
  project. Onset in his early thirties, **revised to a more clinically
  accurate gradual pattern rather than a single tidy incident**: an
  initial, unexpected panic episode with no clear external trigger,
  followed over the next several months by recurring episodes and
  gradually widening anticipatory avoidance, compounding over roughly
  two years into his current, stable pattern. He has built a fully
  functioning professional and personal life around working from home
  over the years since — this reads as competence and adaptation, not
  tragedy, and his professional value to the plot does not depend on
  the condition existing (see Occupation, above) — remove the
  agoraphobia and he is still a skilled records researcher; the
  condition changed *how* he works, not *why* he's good at it or why he
  does it.
- **Physical presence**: Rarely leaves his apartment; his home is his
  entire operational base, organized with real intent (a workspace, not
  clutter-as-metaphor for his condition).
- **Family and cultural background**: LOCKED, Gate 2 — grew up in
  Staten Island; parents both deceased (father, when Calvin was 26;
  mother, three years before story present). One younger sister,
  living in New Jersey, relationship affectionate but attenuated by
  distance and his condition together — she has, over the years,
  stopped expecting him at family gatherings, which he experiences with
  genuine ambivalence (relief and loss both real, neither canceling the
  other). This working estrangement-by-distance, not conflict, is the
  specific reason his professional relationship with "her" (Elena,
  under her records-specialist identity) became more significant to him
  than it might read on its surface — she was one of very few people
  who engaged with him as a full equal in recent years.
- **Education**: A community-college associate's degree in library/
  information science, completed in his early twenties, **before**
  onset — the actual origin of his technical skill, not a post-onset
  adaptation. He has kept current with the field's tools and platforms
  entirely online in the years since, which is an adaptation, but not
  the origin of his competence.
- **Economic situation**: Makes a real, if unglamorous, living from
  freelance data-broker work, increasingly affected by tightening
  industry regulation (`SOURCE_LOG.md` Entry 6) — a grounded, current,
  non-magical source of professional pressure independent of anything
  Mara or Elena does.
- **Living conditions**: Apartment doubles as an operations center —
  multiple monitors, a genuinely organized system, not "organized
  chaos" played for eccentricity.
- **Habits**: Has adapted his working and social life to be fully
  remote-capable, competently, over time — video calls, phone, digital
  collaboration used confidently, not as a workaround he resents.
- **Speech pattern**: Precise, technical, slightly formal in register
  from limited day-to-day in-person social practice — not written as
  awkward-for-comedy.
- **Private vocabulary**: Data-broker and records-research jargon.
- **Contradictions**: Knows more than almost anyone about how to find
  people through data trails — and has, **through a condition he did
  not choose**, adapted his own life so that finding or reaching him in
  person is genuinely difficult (rephrased post-red-team: not
  "deliberately hard to find," which wrongly implied the avoidance
  itself is a chosen strategy rather than the product of an anxiety
  disorder he lives with and has adapted around).
- **Desire**: Maintain his controlled, safe, functional environment;
  solve the intellectual puzzle of who "she" really was, which genuinely
  interests him professionally.
- **Need**: LOCKED post-red-team, no longer hedged — his growth is
  **relational and emotional, not physical**. He lets Mara know
  something real and unguarded about himself, or initiates a form of
  contact (a call he didn't have to make, a piece of trust extended
  without being asked) he wouldn't ordinarily extend — a small, real,
  specific piece of growth. This explicitly does **not** resolve through
  him leaving his apartment or anyone entering it as the marker of
  progress; that "literal" reading is foreclosed here, not left open for
  Gate 4 to default into.
- **Fear**: Being forced into a physical or social situation beyond his
  own control.
- **Shame**: May feel, in unguarded moments, that his condition makes
  him "less" in ways the narrative itself must not endorse or frame as
  true.
- **Secret**: LOCKED, Gate 3 — his past freelance work included building
