# Calvin Moss

STATUS: DRAFT — seed from source brief plus this session's invention;
Gate 2 review not yet run. **Standing instruction from the source
brief, non-negotiable**: his agoraphobia/mobility limitation is handled
without stereotype — not pathologized, not infantilized, not played for
pity or comic relief. He is competent and self-directed; his condition
is a fact of his life, not the narrative's judgment of him.

- **Full name**: Calvin Moss.
- **Age**: 47.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as a multilingual records
  specialist working under another name.
- **Occupation**: Data broker / records researcher, former skip tracer.
  Freelance, entirely remote-capable.
- **Condition**: Agoraphobia and/or mobility limitation (final specific
  choice deferred to Gate 2, made deliberately and with real research,
  not assumed) — he has built a fully functioning professional and
  personal life around working from home; this should read as
  competence and adaptation, not as tragedy.
- **Physical presence**: Rarely leaves his apartment; his home is his
  entire operational base, organized with real intent (a workspace, not
  clutter-as-metaphor for his condition).
- **Family and cultural background**: TBD, Gate 2 — working assumption:
  some degree of estrangement or distance from family, making his
  professional relationship with "her" more significant to him than it
  might read on its surface.
- **Education**: Largely self-taught in data/records work, possibly with
  some formal library-science or data-analysis training.
- **Economic situation**: Makes a real, if unglamorous, living from
  freelance data-broker work.
- **Living conditions**: Apartment doubles as an operations center —
  multiple monitors, a genuinely organized system, not "organized
  chaos" played for eccentricity.
- **Habits**: Has adapted his entire working and social life to be
  fully remote-capable, competently, over time — video calls, phone,
  digital collaboration used confidently, not as a workaround he
  resents.
- **Speech pattern**: Precise, technical, slightly formal in register
  from limited day-to-day in-person social practice — not written as
  awkward-for-comedy.
- **Private vocabulary**: Data-broker and records-research jargon.
- **Contradictions**: Knows more than almost anyone about how to find
  people through data trails — and has built his own life to be
  deliberately hard to find or reach in person.
- **Desire**: Maintain his controlled, safe, functional environment;
  solve the intellectual puzzle of who "she" really was, which genuinely
  interests him professionally.
- **Need**: To let someone into his physical space, literally or
  figuratively, for the first time in a long time — a small, real,
  specific piece of growth, not a "cure" arc.
- **Fear**: Being forced into a physical or social situation beyond his
  own control.
- **Shame**: May feel, in unguarded moments, that his condition makes
  him "less" in ways the narrative itself must not endorse or frame as
  true.
- **Secret**: Has done data-broker work for Palinode-adjacent clients at
  some point without fully understanding the implications at the
  time — a moral gray zone parallel to Jonah Bell's, in a different
  register.
- **False belief**: That staying fully contained and controlled keeps
  him safe from everything, including connection.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Likely conducted almost entirely remotely
  (phone, text, video) for most of the book — itself a useful, distinct
  texture among the seven witness relationships, none of which should
  play out identically.
- **Relationship to Elena**: Intellectual and professional respect — she
  was one of very few people who engaged with him as a full equal.
- **Beginning knowledge**: Holds technical/data evidence relevant to the
  investigation that he hasn't had a reason to examine as evidence
  before.
- **Ending knowledge**: Contributes a specific, technically credible
  piece of the puzzle.
- **Behavioral tells**: TBD, Gate 2 — should be specific and
  individual, not generic "anxious" shorthand.
- **Arc**: Isolated but fully competent → opens up in a small, real way
  → contributes crucial technical evidence on his own terms.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: The character positioned to
  credibly explain and demonstrate data-broker-level vulnerabilities —
  directly relevant to how Palinode's records-based techniques actually
  work (`02_novel_bible/palinode_system.md`) — from an expert, sympathetic
  angle rather than an antagonist's mouth.
- **Stereotype risk**: The single highest-priority representation risk
  in the cast alongside Irena Petrov and Malik Reed — see standing
  instruction above and `00_project/risk_register.md` R-004. Do not
  draft any scene involving him without checking it against real
  agoraphobia/disability representation guidance, not assumption.
- **Research needs**: Real, first-person-informed agoraphobia and
