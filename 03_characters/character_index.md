## The required template (Gate 2 standard — every character file works
toward completing every field below with specific, non-generic content)

Full name · age · role · occupation · physical presence · family and
cultural background · education · economic situation · living
conditions · habits · speech pattern · private vocabulary ·
contradictions · desire · need · fear · shame · secret · false belief ·
relationship to Mara · relationship to Elena · beginning knowledge ·
ending knowledge · lie · reason for lie · behavioral tells · arc · scene
utility · stereotype risk · research needs.

No character exists only as exposition, victim, villain, quirky
neighbor, romantic reward, magical helper, or plot device — every file
below states explicitly what this character does that no other
character could do (their **scene utility**, doubling as their
indispensability check).
