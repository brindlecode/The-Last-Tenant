# Evelyn Price

STATUS: DRAFT — seed from source brief plus this session's invention;
Gate 2 review not yet run.

- **Full name**: Evelyn Price.
- **Age**: 71.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as "Lena Hart."
- **Occupation**: Retired nurse (working assumption: labor-and-delivery/
  maternity background — directly informs why her account of "Lena's"
  pregnancy carries real clinical specificity, not just sentiment).
- **Physical presence**: Still moves like a working nurse — efficient,
  attentive to other people's physical state before her own comfort.
  Age rendered specifically (some slowing, not treated as diminishment).
- **Family and cultural background**: Widow; grown children living
  elsewhere, largely absent from her daily life — a specific, plausible
  reason she had real emotional room to bond deeply with "Lena."
- **Education**: Nursing diploma/degree, decades of hospital practice.
- **Economic situation**: Modest fixed income (pension), long-term
  rent-stabilized tenant.
- **Living conditions**: Same apartment for decades — one of the
  building's most stable residents, which is part of why her testimony
  carries weight with other neighbors.
- **Habits**: Still informally "nurses" people around her — checks on
  neighbors, keeps basic medical supplies on hand. This habit is the
  concrete, plausible reason she has prenatal vitamins, appointment
  cards, blood-pressure notes, and a handmade infant blanket as physical
  evidence — she was actively, semi-professionally caretaking "Lena,"
  not merely socializing with her.
- **Speech pattern**: Clinical precision about physical detail
  (dates, measurements, symptoms) with real warmth underneath — she
  reports facts the way a nurse charts them, which makes her account
  feel unusually credible, including to Mara.
- **Private vocabulary**: Nursing/medical shorthand.
- **Contradictions**: Professionally trained to trust hard clinical
  evidence over sentiment — and the single thing she's most emotionally
  certain of (Lena's pregnancy, her danger) is exactly the part most
  likely to have been at least partly constructed for her benefit. Gate
  3 should decide deliberately whether the pregnancy was fully
  fabricated, fully real, or (most interesting) real but not for the
  reason she was told — not defaulted to the simplest option.
- **Desire**: To have helped Lena successfully. Now, to know she's safe.
- **Need**: To reckon with whether her belief was manipulated or
  genuinely earned — and to arrive at "both, and it still mattered"
  rather than a simple answer either way.
- **Fear**: That she failed Lena when it counted.
- **Shame**: Believes, per the source brief, that she failed Lena —
  carries this as an active, present-tense weight, not backstory color.
- **Secret**: May have privately noticed one inconsistency in Lena's
  account at the time and chosen not to push it, wanting the story — and
  her own usefulness in it — to be true.
- **False belief**: That her clinical instincts are close to infallible
  against being fooled.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Warm, forthcoming, the most emotionally open
  of the seven witnesses — and, potentially, the most devastated once
  the fuller picture emerges.
- **Relationship to Elena**: The deepest caretaking bond among the seven
  accounts; positions her as the clearest on-page vehicle for
  `02_novel_bible/thematic_argument.md`'s "violence of forcing someone
  back into a life they escaped" theme, since exposure risk to "Lena
  Hart" threatens a real, vulnerable outcome.
- **Beginning knowledge**: Believes Lena Hart's account in full.
- **Ending knowledge**: Learns the manufactured elements; must
  reconcile that the *care* was real even where the circumstances
  weren't.
- **Behavioral tells**: TBD, Gate 2.
- **Arc**: Trusting, credible witness → devastated by partial
  fabrication → arrives at the harder, truer position that her care for
  "Lena" was not wasted or foolish.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: Gives the domestic-violence-cover
  storyline its clearest emotional stakes and its most clinically
  credible physical evidence.
- **Stereotype risk**: Elderly-woman-as-naive is explicitly rejected —
  she must read as professionally sharp and clinically credible, someone
  whose belief was reasonable given real evidence, not someone easily
  fooled.
- **Research needs**: Domestic-violence relocation practice (shared with
  `elena_voss.md`); maternity/prenatal nursing detail for authenticity.
  Both queued in `09_research/FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md`; a sensitivity read is
  recommended before this storyline is drafted, per
  `00_project/risk_register.md` R-016.
