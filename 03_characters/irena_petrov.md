# Irena Petrov

STATUS: DRAFT — seed from source brief plus this session's invention;
Gate 2 review not yet run. **Standing instruction, restated from the
source brief and treated as non-negotiable**: do not use her age as
shorthand for unreliability. She may remember sensory and emotional
detail more accurately than dates, which the narrative should treat as
a different, equally valid kind of accuracy — not a lesser one.

- **Full name**: Irena Petrov.
- **Age**: 83.
- **Role**: Witness — the building's oldest long-term resident; likely
  remembers "Anna," a woman living in Bellwether House roughly twenty
  years earlier who looked almost identical to the woman Mara is asking
  about. Per `00_project/gate_1_concept_validation.md` Part B3, the
  working (not yet fully confirmed) answer is that "Anna" was Mara and
  Elena's mother, not Elena — making Irena's testimony a real,
  load-bearing link to the Voss family's actual origin, not atmospheric
  color.
- **Physical presence**: Physically slower, mentally precise where it
  matters to her — written specifically to avoid conflating the two.
- **Family and cultural background**: Emigrated to the United States
  from Russia in the late 1970s/early 1980s (a real, well-documented
  immigration wave to New York), a specific, respectfully researched
  background rather than a generic "old world" placeholder. Full texture
  (region, circumstances of emigration, family left behind or brought)
  to be built with real specificity at Gate 2, not left generic.
- **Education**: Trained in a skilled profession in her country of
  origin (working candidate: teaching), credentials not recognized on
  arrival in the U.S. — a real, common immigrant experience worth
  specific, dignified treatment rather than a passing mention.
- **Economic situation**: Modest, long-term rent-stabilized tenancy —
  among the building's most economically vulnerable residents to any
  disruption.
- **Living conditions**: Same unit for decades; effectively the
  building's living memory.
- **Habits**: Keeps old photographs; notices and holds onto sensory
  detail (light, sound, smell) tied to specific memories with more
  fidelity than she holds onto exact dates.
- **Speech pattern**: An accent and rhythm reflecting her background,
  written with dignity and precision — never rendered as broken or
  comic English. May code-switch briefly into her first language at
  moments of strong feeling.
- **Private vocabulary**: Occasional first-language phrases, used
  naturally, not decoratively.
- **Contradictions**: Forgets some recent, unimportant things; recalls
  decades-old emotionally significant sensory detail with total
  clarity — realistic and, handled well, a genuine craft asset rather
  than a limitation to write around.
- **Desire**: To be believed. To matter — to have her memory count for
  something, after (implicitly) years of people not asking her real
  questions.
- **Need**: Someone (Mara) to actually listen to *how* she remembers,
  not just extract dates from her.
- **Fear**: Being dismissed as unreliable because she's old.
- **Shame**: Minimal — she is one of the story's least morally
  compromised figures, deliberately.
- **Secret**: None in the conventional sense — but she may be sitting on
  the single most structurally important piece of testimony in the
  entire book without knowing its significance, which is a different
  and more poignant thing than a secret.
- **False belief**: That no one actually wants to hear a long story
  from an old woman — which the book should let Mara actively disprove
  through the investigation itself.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Potential for a genuine, warm bond — the
  clearest on-page place for Mara to practice valuing testimony over
  record, ahead of the ending's climactic version of the same lesson.
- **Relationship to Elena**: Minimal direct relationship, by design —
  her significance runs to the mother, not to Elena, a deliberate
  structural choice (see Gate 1 record).
- **Beginning knowledge**: Holds a real, decades-old memory nobody has
  seriously asked her about until Mara's investigation.
- **Ending knowledge**: Her memory is confirmed and recontextualized as
  central rather than tangential.
- **Behavioral tells**: TBD, Gate 2.
- **Arc**: Overlooked, assumed-marginal witness → the source of the
  deepest historical truth in the investigation.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: The only character who connects
  the present-day mystery directly to the Voss family's actual origin;
  embodies "memory versus record" as a lived theme rather than an
  abstraction (`02_novel_bible/thematic_argument.md`).
- **Stereotype risk**: Explicitly flagged in the source brief and
  treated as a hard constraint, not a suggestion — no "wise old woman"
  shorthand, no unreliability-by-age, no reduction to a plot delivery
  device.
- **Research needs**: Specific, respectful research into the real
  immigration wave and experience referenced above before her dialogue
  and backstory are drafted in detail — queued in
  `09_research/FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md`; avoid inventing cultural specifics
  from assumption.
