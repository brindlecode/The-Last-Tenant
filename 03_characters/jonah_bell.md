**Naming-collision fix (resolved this session)**: the source brief names
Jonah's client-identity for Elena "Nora Vale." This collides twice —
"Vale" duplicates Julian Vale's surname within the same cast, and "Nora"
carries separate reuse-risk baggage from the rejected prior branch's
protagonist name (see `00_project/originality_policy.md`). **Elena's
alias in this storyline is renamed to "Dana Kessler."** Use this name in
all future material; treat "Nora Vale" as retired.

- **Full name**: Jonah Bell.
- **Age**: 43.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as "Dana Kessler."
- **Occupation**: Divorce and family-law attorney, small practice or
  boutique firm (unnamed).
- **Physical presence**: Controlled, lawyerly — the specific guardedness
  of someone whose job trains him to say exactly as much as necessary
  and no more, in every context, professional or not.
- **Family and cultural background**: TBD, Gate 2.
- **Education**: Law degree, unnamed institution.
- **Economic situation**: Comfortable professional income.
- **Living conditions**: TBD, Gate 4.
- **Habits**: Works long, discreet hours; compartmentalizes hard.
- **Speech pattern**: Hedged, precise, lawyerly — reflexively qualifies
  statements even in casual conversation.
- **Private vocabulary**: Legal terminology; privilege and
  confidentiality language, sometimes invoked slightly past where it
  actually applies, a tell in itself.
- **Contradictions**: Professionally devoted to helping real people
  escape coercive marriages — personally cagey and controlling about his
  own information in a way that echoes the same instinct for control his
  clients are trying to escape from someone else.
- **Desire**: Protect his clients (including whoever "Dana Kessler"
  helped) and protect himself professionally.
- **Need**: To be direct even when it's professionally risky — a
  smaller-scale version of the book's central "truth versus safety"
  theme, personalized to a man whose entire career is built on managing
  that exact tension for other people.
- **Fear**: Exposure of privileged client work; professional
  compromise.
- **Shame**: Likely suspected "Dana Kessler's" credentials weren't
  entirely what she presented, and used her research work anyway because
  it was good and his clients needed it — a specific, morally gray
  complicity distinct from Gabriel Knox's.
- **Secret**: Knows more about "Dana's" actual legal-research work and
  client contacts than he's told Mara, plausibly to protect ongoing,
  real, vulnerable clients — a genuinely defensible reason to withhold,
  not just self-interest.
- **False belief**: That professional discretion is an adequate
  substitute for honesty, including with himself.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Adversarial by professional instinct — his
  legal training makes him cautious with an investigator, even a
  sympathetic one.
- **Relationship to Elena/"Dana Kessler"**: Professional collaboration
  and mutual respect; both were operating, in different ways, at the
  edge of legality for reasons they each considered justified.
- **Beginning knowledge**: Knows real details of "Dana's" work and
  contacts that could matter to the investigation; withholds them
  initially on privilege/protection grounds.
- **Ending knowledge**: TBD, Gate 3/4 — likely yields information once
  Mara demonstrates she can be trusted with it responsibly.
- **Lie**: Presents his knowledge of "Dana" as more limited than it is.
- **Reason for the lie**: Genuine client-protection duty entangled with
  self-protection — deliberately kept morally mixed, not simple
  self-interest.
- **Behavioral tells**: Over-precise, over-hedged language specifically
  when a question gets close to something he doesn't want to answer;
  reflexively invokes confidentiality even in contexts where it may not
  strictly apply.
- **Arc**: Guarded gatekeeper → forced to weigh client protection against
  helping find Elena → a real, costly disclosure decision.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: Gives the "helping clients
  disappear from coercive spouses" thread real legal procedural texture,
  and is a plausible source of genuine legal jeopardy/friction for
  Mara's investigation (distinct in kind from the police-jurisdiction
  friction Detective Marchetti provides).
- **Stereotype risk**: The "slippery lawyer" trope is a real risk;
  guarded by giving him genuine ethical stakes (protecting real,
  vulnerable current clients) rather than pure self-interest as his
  motive for withholding.
