# Malik Reed

STATUS: Gate 2 COMPLETE. Stereotype-risk and consistency review recorded
in `00_project/gate_2_character_validation.md`. **Standing instruction
from the source brief, non-negotiable**: he must be a credible child,
not an all-knowing plot device. Per `00_project/risk_register.md` R-017,
no drafted scene may place him in direct physical danger from Palinode
without a specific, deliberate story reason logged in `DECISION_LOG.md`
— confirmed this session, per `05_mystery/solution_stress_test.md`, that
no such scenario exists in the locked mystery.

- **Full name**: Malik Reed.
- **Age**: 13.
- **Role**: Witness — knew the missing woman as the person who moved
  through the building's hidden/service routes, understood by nobody
  else his age or, mostly, by the adults either.
- **Occupation**: Eighth grader at a public middle school.
- **Physical presence**: Still growing into his own body — real,
  age-appropriate physical awkwardness and energy, not written as
  smaller-adult composure.
- **Family and cultural background**: LOCKED, Gate 2 — Black American,
  multiple generations rooted in this part of Brooklyn on both sides of
  his family, not recent arrivals, part of his own comfort and specific
  knowledge of the building and block. Parents: **Denise Reed** (mother,
  41, hospital administrative coordinator, primary custodial parent,
  lives with Malik in the building) and **Marcus Reed** (father, 44,
  NYC Transit subway conductor on rotating shifts, lives in another
  Brooklyn neighborhood). Denise and Marcus separated amicably years ago
  over ordinary incompatibility, not conflict; Marcus sees Malik weekly
  on a consistent schedule and coaches his Saturday rec-league
  basketball team — **deliberately built to subvert, not fulfill,** the
  "absent Black father" cliché this file has always flagged as a risk:
  his father's absence from daily household life is a function of work
  schedule and living arrangement, not commitment or presence, and the
  text should never let that distinction blur. Full detail:
  `03_characters/supporting_cast.md`.
- **Education**: Public middle school.
- **Economic situation**: Modest, working household — Denise's
  hospital-administration income is the household's primary support; a
  specific, plausible source of the scheduling gaps (her irregular
  shift-adjacent hours) behind Malik's established habit of unsupervised
  building-exploring after school.
- **Living conditions**: Grew up in the building — knows it the way you
  only know a place you've had the run of since you were small enough
  to fit places adults don't think to check.
- **Habits**: Explores the building's overlooked physical spaces out of
  boredom and genuine curiosity (the former service stairwell, the
  areas around the old dumbwaiter shafts) — a concrete, physical,
  non-mystical explanation for knowing the building's real circulation
  better than most adults.
- **Speech pattern**: Real contemporary teenage speech — direct, often
  deflects a real answer with humor or a shrugged "I don't know" when he
  actually does know, out of habit more than dishonesty. Written with a
  light touch — current enough to feel real, not so slang-dense it reads
  as an adult's idea of how teenagers talk or dates the book
  unnecessarily.
- **Private vocabulary**: Light, age-appropriate gaming/internet
  references — used sparingly.
- **Contradictions**: Performs not caring about adult concerns while
  being, in fact, the most observant person in the building.
- **Desire (surface)**: To be left alone to do his own thing without
  adult interference.
- **Desire (real)**: To be taken seriously by an adult, for once,
  without being condescended to or immediately in trouble.
- **Need**: An adult (Mara) who listens to him as a credible source, not
  a cute anecdote.
- **Fear**: Getting in trouble for where he's been exploring (he's not
  supposed to be in the service stairwell).
- **Shame**: Small, age-appropriate — plausibly embarrassed at having
  been a little scared, once, of something he saw or heard and not told
  anyone.
- **Secret**: Knows a specific physical detail or route relevant to the
  mystery that he hasn't volunteered, because no adult has asked him the
  right question, and because volunteering it means admitting where
  he's been.
- **False belief**: That adults either won't believe him or will only
  respond by shutting down his exploring.
- **Relationship to Mara**: Starts wary/dismissive of adult attention,
  becomes a genuine, credible ally once she demonstrably takes him
  seriously.
- **Relationship to Elena**: LOCKED, Gate 3 — noticed, not known; his
  sightings of "a woman" using the service routes cluster in the final
  two to three months before the disappearance, coinciding with Elena's
  increased trips to consolidate her hidden evidence cache
  (`05_mystery/elena_timeline.md`). Curious and a little awed rather
  than a deep relationship; he doesn't know her, he's noticed her.
- **Beginning knowledge**: Real, physical, specific knowledge of routes
  and timing nobody has asked him about.
- **Ending knowledge**: Vindicated as a real source once Mara
  cross-references his timeline against Gabriel Knox's maintenance logs
  (`05_mystery/clue_register.md` #10) — confirmed to have no further
  role in the climax itself, consistent with the R-017 constraint above.
- **Behavioral tells**: **AMENDED post-red-team** (the original tell,
  "taps a rhythm," collided with Teo Marchetti's sternum-tap): bounces a
  knee or picks at a loose thread or shoelace when deciding whether to
  volunteer something; answers a direct question about where he's been
  with a slightly-too-quick "nowhere" before, if pressed kindly rather
  than sharply, actually telling the truth — ordinary-kid tells, not a
  device.
- **Arc**: Overlooked kid → credible witness who provides real, grounded
  information → treated, by the end, as someone whose observations
  mattered.
- **Scene utility / indispensability**: Literal, physical knowledge of
  the building's hidden circulation (`02_novel_bible/bellwether_house.md`)
  that no adult resident has reason to possess, and the specific,
  confirmed timing that lets Mara cross-reference his account against
  Gabriel Knox's logs — two independent evidence sources corroborating
  each other, a real fair-play mechanism.
- **Stereotype risk**: Both "all-knowing plot-device kid" and
  "endangered child used for stakes" explicitly rejected — confirmed
  this session that no scene in the locked mystery requires either.
- **Research needs**: Age-appropriate dialogue calibration remains a
  low-priority craft-calibration item (`FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md` item 12) —
  not a plausibility concern.
