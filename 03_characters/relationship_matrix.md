## Core relationships

| Pair | Nature | Key tension |
|---|---|---|
| Mara ↔ Elena | Sisters | Proof vs. belief; unequal memory of the same past — see `02_novel_bible/thematic_argument.md` |
| Mara ↔ Julian Vale | Investigator ↔ architect of her own origin | He regards her as evidence he was right; she doesn't yet know to resent this |
| Mara ↔ Adrienne Shaw | Mentee ↔ mentor, professionally | Real mentorship entangled with institutional compromise |
| Mara ↔ Teo Marchetti | Civilian expert ↔ detective of record | Jurisdictional friction → grudging alliance, platonic |
| Mara ↔ Gabriel Knox | Investigator ↔ reluctant, complicit witness | His silence is the wall she has to get through first |
| Elena ↔ Julian Vale | Protégé (early) ↔ whistleblower-in-progress (late) | His betrayal is personal to him, not only operational |
| Elena ↔ the seven witnesses | Multiple performed identities ↔ real relationships | Each relationship is simultaneously fabricated-in-origin and emotionally real |
| Mara's mother ("Anna," per Irena's memory) ↔ Bellwether House / Palinode | Original client, decades ago | The generational echo the whole book is built on |

## Witness relationships (each distinct in kind, not a repeated shape)

| Witness | Relationship to "Elena" | Distinguishing quality |
|---|---|---|
| Evelyn Price | Deep caretaking bond ("Lena Hart") | Most emotionally invested; clinical evidence |
| Jonah Bell | Professional collaboration ("Dana Kessler") | Mutual edge-of-legality respect |
| Irena Petrov | Minimal/indirect (her real bond was to the mother) | Structurally the most important, emotionally the most understated |
| Malik Reed | Noticed, not known | Physical/observational only |
| Rina Cho | Research subject/contact | Professional fascination, documentary evidence |
| Calvin Moss | Remote professional respect | Conducted mostly at a distance, technical evidence |
| Gabriel Knox | Complicit knowledge, incomplete relationship | The only witness whose silence is itself culpable |

**Design rule maintained across all seven**: no two witness relationships
share the same emotional shape or evidence type — checked here
explicitly so Gate 4 scene-writing doesn't accidentally flatten them into
variations on one relationship.
