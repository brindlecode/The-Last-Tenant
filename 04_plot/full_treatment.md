# Full Treatment

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 4 work.

## What belongs here

A complete, beat-by-beat prose treatment of the entire novel (typically
several thousand words) — the connective tissue between
`five_page_synopsis.md`'s summary level and `chapter_map.md`'s per-
chapter breakdown. Every scene's function, not just every part's
function, gets stated.

## Why it isn't written yet

A full treatment written before Gate 3 (mystery timelines, clue
register, fairness audit) closes would lock scene-level plot specifics
against a mystery architecture that hasn't been stress-tested yet — see
`00_project/production_workflow.md`'s rule against planning-workflow
looseness bleeding into premature specificity. Writing this now would
mean either re-writing it substantially once Gate 3 finishes, or
(worse) letting Gate 3 quietly conform to a treatment that was never
actually validated.

## Prerequisite

`05_mystery/` Gate 3 closure (all six timelines, clue register, red-
herring register, reveal ladder, fairness audit) and `act_structure.md`
/ `sequence_structure.md`.

## What exists in the meantime

`04_plot/master_synopsis.md` and `five_page_synopsis.md` carry the
current best-confidence shape of the whole book, with open items
explicitly marked, and should be used for orientation until this file
is built.
