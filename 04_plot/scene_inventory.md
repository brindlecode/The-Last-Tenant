# Scene Inventory

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 4 work.

## What belongs here

Every individual scene in the novel (finer-grained than the chapter
level — a chapter may hold more than one scene), each tagged with its
primary conflict (cross-referenced to `02_novel_bible/conflict_matrix.md`),
its POV, and what it changes (a fact learned, a relationship shifted, a
decision made). A scene that doesn't change anything is a candidate for
cutting — this inventory is the tool that makes that check possible at
scale.

## Prerequisite

`chapter_map.md`.
