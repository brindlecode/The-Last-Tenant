# Sequence Structure

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 4 work.

## What belongs here

Mid-level structure between acts and chapters — the novel broken into
its constituent dramatic sequences (typically 4–8 chapters each, built
around a specific question or goal Mara is pursuing), bridging
`act_structure.md`'s three-part view and `chapter_map.md`'s per-chapter
view. For this book, sequences will likely track the seven witnesses'
accounts as a partially-sequential structure in Part II (see
`03_characters/relationship_matrix.md`'s note that each witness
relationship is deliberately distinct in kind) alongside the parallel
personal-history sequence (the mother, the childhood event, Mara's own
origin).

## Prerequisite

`act_structure.md`; `05_mystery/reveal_ladder.md` (sequences should be
built around what's revealed when, not just around scenes).
