# Clue Density Map

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 3 work, requires `clue_register.md` to be
populated first.

## What belongs here

A per-chapter (once chapters are mapped) count and weight of clues
planted/reinforced/paid off, used to catch two specific failure modes:
chapters overloaded with clue-planting at the expense of scene/character
work (tension with the emotional-core protection clause,
`02_novel_bible/thematic_argument.md`), and long stretches with no clue
activity at all, which risk making the eventual solution feel
under-seeded. Given this book's unusually large evidence set (seven
witnesses, six timelines), density tracking matters more here than in a
simpler mystery structure.

## Prerequisite

`clue_register.md`; `04_plot/chapter_map.md`.
