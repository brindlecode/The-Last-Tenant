# Elena's Operational Timeline

STATUS: LOCKED (Gate 3 complete). Elena-specific filtered view of
`objective_truth_timeline.md`, expanded with character detail from
`03_characters/elena_voss.md`. Confidential-adjacent per that file's
header.

- **Childhood (23 years ago, age 9, through early adulthood)**: Grows up
  as "Elena Voss" alongside Mara. In her early twenties, finds a
  document among their mother Anna's kept papers (exact document TBD,
  Gate 4/drafting — a working candidate is a piece of Peter Marsh's
  original correspondence, kept despite the danger of keeping it,
  because Anna could not bring herself to destroy the last thing in his
  handwriting) that starts her working out more of the family's true
  origin than Mara ever does.
- **~8–6 years before story present**: Theater/conservatory training;
  inconsistent acting work. During a financially lean period, recruited
  into paid Palinode-adjacent continuity/performance work, initially
  without understanding the organization's full scope — the work reads,
  at first, as legitimate crisis-relocation-adjacent performance
  contracting.
- **Operative period (~6–2.5 years before story present)**: Works as a
  continuity operative/profile-builder/performer for Palinode. Genuinely
  skilled — real, significant competence, not naivety.
- **The complicity that turns her (~4–3 years before story present,
  consequence discovered ~2.5 years before story present)**: Builds a
  full continuity identity for a paying client relocating to escape a
  credible abuse allegation tied to his position running a private
  youth program. Roughly a year later, learns through a contact in her
  performance/documentary-adjacent world that he re-offended under the
  new identity. This is her whistleblower trigger: a specific, addressed
  harm, not abstract guilt — she cannot undo it without exposing Palinode
  and herself, and she does not idealize simple total exposure as the
  answer, because she already understands, from this experience, that
  indiscriminate exposure would also destroy people Palinode genuinely
  protected. This lived, morally compromised understanding is what Mara
  has to learn to trust at the climax (`04_plot/final_ending.md`).
- **Turn to investigation (~2 years before story present)**: Begins
  quietly gathering proof against Palinode generally — leverage, penance,
  and a wish to find a way to stop the organization without destroying
  everyone it had also protected. In the course of this, recognizes her
  own family's history from the inside and identifies Bellwether House
  as the site of their original processing.
- **Bellwether House campaign (roughly 14–18 months before story
  present, through the disappearance)**: Enters as, functionally, all
  seven witness-facing identities, staggered across the window —
  Gabriel Knox and Irena Petrov earliest (permanent residents, easiest
  ongoing access); Evelyn Price ("Lena Hart") and Jonah Bell ("Dana
  Kessler") developing through the middle of the window; Rina Cho's
  documentary-research contact and Calvin Moss's remote records
  relationship established later, closer to when her evidence-gathering
  intensified; Malik Reed's sightings clustering in the final two to
  three months as she made more frequent trips to consolidate her
  hidden cache. This is not primarily an attempt to hide — it is a
  deliberate, patient, redundant evidence-gathering method that mirrors
  Palinode's own core principle (multiple independent-seeming
  confirmations, each one real) back at the organization that taught it
  to her.
- **The "Lena Hart" pregnancy (resolves `evidence_provenance.md`'s open
  item)**: Real, but not for the reason Evelyn Price was told. Elena
  experienced a real pregnancy loss early in the "Lena Hart"
  relationship, which she wove into the existing "fleeing an abusive
  partner" persona rather than disclose the truth, because reworking
  the persona at that point would have risked its credibility. Evelyn's
  physical evidence (vitamins, appointment cards, blood-pressure notes,
  the handmade blanket) is genuinely real; so was the loss it responded
  to.
- **The hidden cache**: Elena hides her evidence archive inside
  Apartment 4C itself, the safest available location given its
  official off-record status. Prepares a contingency envelope addressed
  to Mara ("You were the first tenant") as a dead-man's-switch message,
  in case she is stopped before delivering the proof in person — its
  literal meaning is that Apartment 4C is where the family's false
  "Voss" identity, and Mara's specifically, began.
- **Discovery and disappearance (final weeks/days before story
  present)**: Gabriel Knox's self-protective report alerts Palinode.
  Sensing she has little time left, Elena leaves Mara the fragmented
  voicemail — fragmented because she is speaking under real time
  pressure and fear of over-disclosure on a recorded line — then
  disappears. Julian Vale personally directs her move to a controlled,
  non-violent containment site.
- **Disappearance window (story present, roughly five to six weeks)**:
  Held at the containment site, isolated from her phone, ID, and
  outside contact, under a mix of Julian's personal persuasion and
  soft coercion. Not harmed, per Palinode's operational self-
  preservation, but genuinely damaged by the accumulated psychological
  cost of the confinement layered onto years of performed identity and
  the specific weight of her own complicity.
- **Discovery**: Located by Mara in Part III, alive, carrying that
  accumulated cost. Her reunion with Mara is not simple relief — she has
  to account for the youth-program client's re-offense, for the seven
  relationships she built and is now leaving broken, and for having used
  her own sister's history as investigative leverage even while loving
  her. See `03_characters/elena_voss.md` and `04_plot/final_ending.md`.
