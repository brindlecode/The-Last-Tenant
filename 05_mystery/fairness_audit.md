# Fairness Audit

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 3 work, requires `clue_register.md` and

## What belongs here

A systematic check that the solution is inferable before the climax but
not obvious — for every major reveal, confirming a reasonably attentive
reader had access to the facts needed to anticipate it, without the
answer being telegraphed early. Directly enforces
`02_novel_bible/genre_contract.md`'s core promise to the reader.

## Working principle already locked (see `genre_contract.md`)

Every one of the seven witnesses' accounts must be independently
verifiable as evidence-based rather than a lie to catch — the "fairness"
in this book's specific case is about pattern-recognition across
genuine partial truths, not about spotting who's lying. The audit, once
it runs, should test specifically for this rather than generic
whodunit fairness criteria.
