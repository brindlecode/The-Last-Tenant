# Mara's Knowledge Timeline

STATUS: Part-level granularity LOCKED (Gate 3 complete at this depth).
Full chapter-by-chapter progression remains explicit Gate 4 work,

not to begin. This file replaces `knowledge_matrix.md`'s beginning-of-
book-only snapshot with a three-part progression, which is the correct
and complete Gate 3 deliverable in the absence of a chapter map — Gate 4

knowledge content.

## Beginning of Part I

Baseline per `03_characters/knowledge_matrix.md`: knows nothing of
Elena's Palinode entanglement, her own manufactured origin, "Anna," or
Apartment 4C's real occupancy history.

## End of Part I (through the Apartment 4C climax)

Knows: Elena's last confirmed address is Apartment 4C; the unit is
officially vacant twelve years on paper; seven residents independently,
genuinely remember a woman matching Elena under seven different names,
none of them lying; Gabriel Knox is withholding something specific
rather than simply forgetful. **Newly knows, at the Part I climax**:
Elena hid a cache of identity documents, all bearing her face, none
sharing a name, inside Apartment 4C — and left an envelope addressed
specifically to Mara reading "You were the first tenant." **Does not yet
know** what this means, that Palinode exists as a named organization, or
anything about her own origin.

## Through Part II

Progressively learns, in this order (exact chapter sequencing is Gate
4 work): the existence and scope of the Palinode Continuity Group;
that Apartment 4C's "vacancy" is a deliberate paperwork mechanism, not
an absence of record; that Irena Petrov's memory of "Anna" is
significant rather than an old woman's confusion, and very likely
describes her own mother; that "Voss" is not the family's original
surname; that Adrienne Shaw has suppressed Palinode-linked claim
patterns for years (**Part II betrayal, ~60–70% mark**: Adrienne
restricts Mara's professional access, using individually defensible
institutional grounds); that Julian Vale is willing to meet her directly
and characterizes Palinode's history with real, unsettling credibility;
that he regards her as Palinode's "greatest success." **Does not yet
know**, through most of Part II: her father's real name, employer, or
cause of death; the specific mechanism of Elena's disappearance; that
Elena was a Palinode operative, not purely an investigator from outside.
**Learns Elena's operative history and partial complicity** later in
Part II, materially complicating her sympathy for her sister before
Elena is found — required by `risk_register.md` R-011.

## Through Part III, to the climax

Learns (working order, Gate 4 to place at chapter level): her father's
real name (Peter Marsh), real profession, and real cause of death —
via the buried regulatory-complaint paper trail
(`evidence_provenance.md`) and, ideally reinforcing rather than
duplicating that discovery, a direct conversation with her mother, Anna
Voss. Locates Elena's containment site. At the climax: confronts Julian
Vale directly; learns (or has confirmed) the full mechanism of Elena's
capture, including Gabriel Knox's role as the specific trigger. Chooses
to act on Elena's judgment over her own fully verified evidence on which
Palinode-protected identities to expose.

## Ending state

Knows the full truth of Palinode, her own origin, her father's fate,
and what happened to Elena. Critically, per `04_plot/final_ending.md`,
she also comes to know — and act on — the difference between having
proof and having earned trust, which is the book's actual argument
resolved through her knowledge state changing, not just her plot
position.
