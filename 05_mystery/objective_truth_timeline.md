## Chronology

- **~35–40 years before story present**: Julian Vale, working in
  government-adjacent victim/witness relocation, becomes convinced
  official protection is too narrow. Founds the earliest version of what
  becomes Palinode — genuinely altruistic at this stage. See
  `02_novel_bible/palinode_system.md`.
- **~30 years before story present (approx.)**: A senior partner with
  financial/insurance-systems expertise joins, giving Palinode its
  records/credit-system sophistication (not yet a built character — see
  `03_characters/supporting_cast.md`).
- **Sometime in this early-to-middle period**: Funding pressure begins
  the shift from purely altruistic cases toward paying clients,
  including clients escaping accountability rather than danger. Mission
  drift, not a single corrupting event — see `palinode_system.md`.
- **~20+ years before story present**: Mara and Elena's mother,
  fleeing danger connected to the girls' father (external
  criminal/institutional danger — selected over a domestic-violence
  framing specifically to avoid tonal repetition with two other threads;
  see `00_project/gate_1_concept_validation.md` Part B3), is processed
  through Palinode **at Bellwether House specifically**. The family
  emerges with the surname "Voss" (assigned, not original). Irena
  Petrov, then a newer resident herself, meets and remembers her as
  "Anna." **Open**: exact year, exact nature of the father's danger, the
  father's fate (left behind/died/arrested/unknown), the family's
  original surname — all deliberately deferred to Gate 3, not
  defaulted.
- **Mara grows up as "Mara Voss"** with no knowledge her identity was
  constructed. Elena grows up alongside her, gradually working out more
  than Mara does about their origin (mechanism/timing TBD, Gate 3).
- **Elena's early adulthood**: Theater training; inconsistent acting
  work; recruited into paid Palinode-adjacent performance/continuity
  work during a lean financial period, initially without full
  understanding of the organization's scope. See
  `03_characters/elena_voss.md` and `elena_timeline.md`.
- **~12 years before story present**: The last official paper record
  touching Apartment 4C — a synthetic/deceased-identity lease held via a
  Palinode-linked shell entity — formally lapses. No new filing occurs
  from this point forward; this is the specific origin of the "twelve
  years vacant" official record. See
  `02_novel_bible/bellwether_house.md`.
- **Some point before Elena's disappearance**: Elena turns from operative
  toward whistleblower-in-progress, having learned enough to threaten
  Palinode meaningfully. Trigger not yet built — Gate 3 item.
- **The seven witness relationships** (Evelyn Price/"Lena Hart"; Jonah
  Bell/"Dana Kessler"; the Rina Cho and Calvin Moss contacts; Malik
  Reed's observations; Gabriel Knox's complicity) occur across an
  unspecified span leading up to Elena's disappearance — exact sequencing
  and duration TBD, Gate 3, needed before `evidence_provenance.md` can be
  fully dated.
- **Immediately before story present**: Elena leaves Mara the fragmented
  voicemail, then disappears. **Open**: what specifically happens to her
  in this window (captured, in hiding, injured, or some combination) —
  the central mystery the book's Part I–III arc resolves; must remain
  genuinely undetermined in-world until Gate 3 locks it, and must remain
  hidden from the reader per the reveal ladder once built.
- **Story present**: Mara's investigation begins.
