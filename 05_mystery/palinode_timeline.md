## Founding and drift (see `palinode_system.md` for full detail)

Founded ~35–40 years before story present by Julian Vale out of
government-adjacent relocation work; joined early by a financial-systems

altruistic cases toward paying clients, some legitimate, increasingly
some not.

## The Voss family case

~20+ years before story present: processes Mara and Elena's mother (and,
per open items, possibly the girls directly or possibly just the
mother initially) at Bellwether House. This is very likely **not** a
random client case but one Julian Vale has personal investment in — his
later regard for Mara as "Palinode's greatest success" (per
`03_characters/julian_vale.md`) only makes sense if he tracked the
outcome, which implies either personal involvement in the case or a
standing practice of monitoring outcomes generally. **Gate 3 decision
needed**: which of these it is, and whether Julian handled the Voss case
personally or it reached him only later.

## Apartment 4C as an ongoing site

~12 years before story present: the last paper lease on Apartment 4C
lapses; from this point the unit operates entirely off official record.
**Open, high-priority Gate 3 question**: is Bellwether House Palinode's
single most active or most important continuity site (because of its
history with the Voss family), or one working site among several,
reactivated in particular by Elena's own return to it? The answer
changes how much operational activity should be visible in the building
during the story-present timeline, and needs to be decided before
`bellwether_house.md`'s cast-placement and clue material can be finalized
with confidence.
