# Reader Knowledge Timeline

STATUS: NOT STARTED — Gate 3/4 work, requires `chapter_map.md`.

## What belongs here

A chapter-by-chapter record of what the *reader* knows, which is not
always identical to what Mara knows — per
`01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md`'s fair-play rule, the reader must
never know *less* than Mara about anything she's personally concluded
(no artificial narration-level withholding), but the reader may
legitimately know things Mara doesn't yet (via document inserts, Elena
fragments, or dramatic irony built fairly from material already shown).
This file is what makes that distinction checkable rather than
instinctive.

## Relationship to mara_knowledge_timeline.md

The two files should be near-identical for most of the book, with
deliberate, logged divergence points — every divergence needs a stated
reason (e.g., "reader sees the Ch. 1 document insert Mara doesn't revisit
until Ch. 9") rather than accumulating as an unexamined side effect of
drafting.

## Prerequisite

`04_plot/chapter_map.md`; `mara_knowledge_timeline.md`.
