# Reader Knowledge Timeline

STATUS: Part-level granularity LOCKED (Gate 3 complete at this depth).
Full chapter-level version remains Gate 4 work, requiring
`04_plot/chapter_map.md`. Per `01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md`'s
fair-play rule, the reader must never know *less* than Mara about
anything she's personally concluded — this file tracks the deliberate,
logged exceptions where the reader legitimately knows more.

## General relationship to `mara_knowledge_timeline.md`

Near-identical for most of the book. Deliberate divergence points below,
each with a stated reason, per this file's own standing requirement.

## Divergence 1 — Elena's chapters/fragments (if Gate 4 uses them)

`00_project/project_charter.md` allows "brief Elena fragments where
justified." If Gate 4 elects to use any (not yet decided — a Gate 4
choice, not made this session), the reader would learn pieces of
Elena's operative history, her whistleblower trigger, or the reality of
her containment **before** Mara does. **Recommendation, not a lock**:
use fragments sparingly and mostly in Part III, once Mara is actively
searching for her, so reader-ahead-of-Mara dramatic irony intensifies
tension toward the climax rather than deflating the Part I–II mystery
engine by resolving "what happened to Elena" too early for the reader
even while Mara is still working it out. If Gate 4 elects not to use
Elena fragments at all, this divergence point doesn't exist and the two
timelines stay identical throughout — also a fully valid choice per
`01_author_bible/narrative_distance.md`.

## Divergence 2 — Document inserts (interview transcripts, claim notes,
building records)

Per `project_charter.md`'s allowance for these forms: where a document
insert is shown in full (e.g., a fragment of Gabriel Knox's maintenance
log, a property record), the reader sees its complete content at the
moment of the insert, while Mara may not fully register or revisit its
significance until later. This is standard, fair dramatic irony (the
reader re-reads a detail once its meaning becomes clear) rather than
narration-level withholding, and should be used deliberately for at
least the maintenance-log evidence and the property/shell-entity
paper trail, both established as real, dated, hard evidence in
`evidence_provenance.md`.

## Divergence 3 — Irena Petrov's account

Per her established stereotype-risk guard
(`03_characters/irena_petrov.md`): the narration must not let the
reader share any character's dismissal of her memory as unreliable-
because-elderly. The reader should be positioned, from her first scene,
to take her account exactly as seriously as `gate_3_mystery_validation.md`
item 22 confirms it deserves — this is a narrative-stance requirement,
not a knowledge-content divergence, but is logged here because it's the
mechanism by which the reader can, if the prose is handled correctly,
recognize the significance of her testimony slightly ahead of Mara's own
explicit realization, which is a legitimate, earned form of reader-ahead
dramatic irony distinct from being told outright.

## What must NOT diverge

The central mysteries — what happened to Elena, the Voss family's true
origin, Palinode's scope, the Part II betrayal — must land for the
reader at the same time they land for Mara, or later, never earlier,
except through the two logged, deliberate mechanisms above. No
narrator-level knowledge of the objective truth (`objective_truth_timeline.md`)
may leak into prose ahead of what Mara or a legitimate document/
fragment insert has actually established on the page.

## Standing rule

Any new divergence point introduced during Gate 4 or drafting must be
added here, with a stated reason, in the same session that introduces
it — an unexamined divergence is exactly the failure mode this file
exists to prevent.
