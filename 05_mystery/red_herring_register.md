## 1. Gabriel Knox as apparent primary villain

**Status**: Confirmed as a fair red herring. His evasiveness is real; his
complicity is real but smaller in scope than his behavior initially
suggests (paid to not-notice ordinary activity, not an active fraud
author) — **and** he is, separately, the specific human trigger for
Elena's capture, via a self-protective report that is a distinct,
escalated act beyond his original arrangement
(`gate_3_mystery_validation.md` item 16). This actually *sharpens* the
red herring rather than resolving it simply: a reader/Mara suspecting
him of being more central than he is will be both wrong (he didn't
architect anything) and right (his choice genuinely caused Elena's
capture) in different, specific ways — a more interesting outcome than
either a clean exoneration or a clean villain reveal.

## 2. Adrienne Shaw as directly Palinode-complicit

**Status**: Confirmed as a fair red herring, now resolved. She is
**not** one of Julian Vale's knowingly-managed long-term assets — her
compromise is self-generated (years of generalized, self-protective
not-quite-knowing about suppressed claim patterns), and Palinode-linked
pressure reaches her reactively, once, rather than her being a standing
conspirator (`gate_3_mystery_validation.md` item 9). The "directly
complicit" reading remains fair for most of the book because her
actual behavior (suppressing patterns, later restricting Mara's access)
is externally indistinguishable from deliberate complicity until her
own POV-adjacent scene (Gate 4 to place) clarifies the distinction.

## 3. Dr. Priya Ansari as "the real power behind Julian Vale"

**Status**: Now a built, named character (`03_characters/supporting_cast.md`),
evaluated as a red-herring candidate per this register's own prior
flag. **Verdict**: usable but optional — she genuinely does hold
technical authority Julian lacks, which is real and could support a
misdirection beat ("who is actually in charge here"), but the locked
mystery does not require her as an active present-day operational
figure (she is semi-retired and deliberately distant from the response
to Elena/Mara). Gate 4 may use her as a red herring if a scene wants
one; the mystery does not depend on it.

## 4. Teo Marchetti's procedural caution

**Status**: Confirmed as a fair red herring, unaffected by this
session's other locks. Consistent with his established false belief
(procedure and truth are the same thing) and costs him narrative
sympathy for real, motivated reasons — not authorial trickery.

## 5. The twelve-year vacancy record itself (new this session)

**Status**: New candidate, added per `clue_register.md` #1. The record
is real and honestly reported; it misleads by being true but
irrelevant to present-day occupancy (it marks an unconnected lapsed
lease, not the family's own decades-earlier history in the unit). Fair
because the misreading is Mara's own reasonable initial inference, not
a narrative lie.
