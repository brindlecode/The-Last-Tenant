## What belongs here

Once drafting begins, this file (or a per-chapter/per-part series of
files following this naming convention — finalize the convention at
Gate 5) records the Continuity Audit step of every chapter's pass
through `00_project/production_workflow.md`'s drafting workflow: new
prose checked against `canonical_facts.md`, `ages_and_dates.md`,
`locations.md`, `physical_objects.md`, `injuries_and_health.md`,
`clothing_and_appearance.md`, and every prior chapter, with findings and
resolutions recorded, not just a pass/fail stamp.
