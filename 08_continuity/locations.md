## Standing rules (from `setting_bible.md`)

Real NYC/Brooklyn general geography and public institutions (agency
types, real neighborhood names used generically for orientation) may be
referenced. No real specific address, business, or building is ever
used or implied. New invented locations should stay consistent with
Bellwether House's general placement (brownstone/pre-war central
Brooklyn, in the general Fort Greene/Clinton Hill/Prospect Heights
vicinity, not pinned to one exact block).
