# Legal Risk Notes

STATUS: DRAFT — consolidates and expands `00_project/risk_register.md`'s
legal/sensitivity items (R-015 through R-018) with research-specific
guardrails. Cross-check both files together; neither is complete alone.

## 1. Operational harm (identity fraud how-to risk)

**The risk**: any depiction of Palinode's methods detailed enough to
function as real, actionable instruction for manufacturing a synthetic
identity, disappearing undetected, or defeating a specific financial/
insurance control.

**The rule**: `02_novel_bible/palinode_system.md` characterizes methods
at the category level (what kind of vulnerability, what it costs, what
human dependency it relies on) and explicitly bans one-command/
omniscient mechanisms. `00_project/originality_policy.md` §4 is the
standing policy. Every scene drafted involving Palinode's actual
tradecraft must be checked against this before being finalized — not
just once at Gate 3, but at every future chapter's Factual Audit
(`00_project/production_workflow.md`) once drafting begins.

**Research implication**: when researching real synthetic-identity-fraud
mechanics (`FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md` items), the goal is *understanding
enough to write convincingly at the category level*, not accumulating
technique-level detail the manuscript would then need to actively avoid
using. Research notes themselves should stay at the same level of
generality the manuscript will use.

## 2. Domestic violence relocation content

**The risk**: inaccurate or (worse) inadvertently unsafe-reading
depiction of how real DV relocation and protective services work,
which could mislead a reader in a similar real situation.

**The rule**: research required before drafting (not just before
publication) per `FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md` item 3 (sensitivity read,
required, not yet done) and item 4 (NY State program specifics); the
general mechanism is now sourced (`SOURCE_LOG.md` Entry 5), but a
dedicated sensitivity read for Evelyn Price's and Jonah Bell's related
storylines remains outstanding and is not satisfied by general research.
Fiction is not obligated to be a procedural manual, but should not
actively misinform on a topic with real safety stakes for real readers.

## 3. Minor in proximity to danger

**The risk**: Malik Reed (13) placed in physical danger from Palinode
as a cheap stakes-raising device.

**The rule**: per `00_project/risk_register.md` R-017, no drafted scene
may place him in direct physical danger from Palinode without a
specific, deliberate story reason logged in `DECISION_LOG.md` at the
time it's written — this is a drafting-time gate, not a publication-time
review item.

## 4. Real-institution confusion

**The risk**: a fictional insurance company (Meridian Assurance Group),
a fictional antagonist organization (Palinode), and a fictional building
(Bellwether House) being misread as claims about real entities.

**The rule**: no real company, building, or address is ever used —
already policy (`CLAUDE.md`, `originality_policy.md`); low residual risk
as long as this is maintained.

## 5. Real-person and real-address avoidance generally

Standard fiction practice, restated as an explicit legal-risk item
rather than left implicit: no real named private individuals; invented
streets/buildings within real, generically-referenced neighborhoods
(`02_novel_bible/setting_bible.md`).

## 6. Title uniqueness (not a legal risk, flagged here for completeness)

Titles are generally not protectable IP in the U.S.; the uniqueness
concern tracked in `00_project/risk_register.md` R-013 and
`12_publishing/title_review.md` is a market-discoverability issue, not a
legal exposure — noted here only so it isn't mistakenly escalated as a
legal question later.

## Standing instruction

Any new legal or sensitivity concern identified during future research
or drafting gets added here immediately, cross-referenced in
`risk_register.md`, in the same session it's noticed — not deferred to
a future audit pass.
