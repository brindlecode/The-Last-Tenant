## Method

Per `CLAUDE.md` and `00_project/originality_policy.md` §5: no factual
claim used to ground this novel's real-world texture is invented and
presented as fact. Every such claim is either sourced (citation, access
date, confidence level, effect on story, and an explicit fictionalization
decision, in `SOURCE_LOG.md`) or logged as an open question in
`FACT_CHECK_QUEUE.md`. "This is probably how it works" is never
sufficient basis for a claim the manuscript will rely on.
