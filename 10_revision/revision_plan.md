# Revision Plan

`07_manuscript/`.

## The planned revision pipeline (for when it applies)

Once a full draft exists, revision proceeds in passes, each producing
its own report file in this directory:

1. **`developmental_edit.md`** — big-picture: does the story work? Does
   the concept validated at Gate 1 actually deliver across 46 chapters?
2. **`structural_edit.md`** — checks the delivered manuscript against
   `04_plot/act_structure.md`, `chapter_map.md`, and the required
   milestones.
3. **`character_edit.md`** — checks every character against
   `03_characters/character_arc_matrix.md`; flags any arc that didn't
   actually land on the page.
4. **`suspense_edit.md`** — checks the mystery's fairness and tension
   against `05_mystery/fairness_audit.md` and `04_plot/tension_curve.md`
   as actually delivered, not just as planned.
5. **`dialogue_edit.md`** — checks against
   `01_author_bible/dialogue_guide.md`.
6. **`line_edit.md`** — sentence-level craft, per
   `01_author_bible/prose_style_guide.md` and `style_audit_checklist.md`,
   run across the whole manuscript rather than per-chapter.
7. **`copy_edit.md`** — mechanical correctness.
8. **`proofread.md`** — final pass before considering the manuscript
   submission-ready.
9. **`repetition_report.md`** — cross-manuscript repetition check (the
   full-book version of the per-chapter/per-audit checks already run
   during drafting).
10. **`cliche_report.md`** — full-manuscript check against
    `01_author_bible/banned_cliches.md`.
11. **`pacing_report.md`** — full-manuscript version of
    `04_plot/tension_curve.md` / `emotional_curve.md` checks.
12. **`sensitivity_review.md`** — dedicated pass on every character/
    storyline flagged in `00_project/risk_register.md` (Evelyn Price/DV
    content, Irena Petrov, Malik Reed, Calvin Moss) — ideally involving
    real outside sensitivity reads, not just self-review.
13. **`final_quality_report.md`** — consolidated sign-off against every
    item in `00_project/definition_of_done.md`'s "a revision pass"
    standard.

## Prerequisite

A complete or substantially complete manuscript draft in
`07_manuscript/`.
