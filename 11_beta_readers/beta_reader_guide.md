# Beta Reader Guide

STATUS: DRAFT — usable framework written now since it derives from
already-locked Gate 0/1 material; **not yet deployable** since no

## What beta readers will be asked, once there's a manuscript

Questions are built directly from this project's own stated goals, so
feedback is checked against what the book is trying to do, not against
generic "did you like it":

1. **Fair play**: Did you feel like you had a real chance to guess the
   shape of the truth before the reveals, or did it feel like
   information was arbitrarily withheld? (Tests
   `02_novel_bible/genre_contract.md`'s core promise.)
2. **Emotional core**: If asked to describe what the book is "about" in
   one sentence, do you name the sisters' relationship, or only the
   mystery plot? (Tests `thematic_argument.md`'s protection clause
   directly — this is the single most important beta question in the
   whole guide.)
3. **Palinode**: Did Julian Vale and Palinode feel like a real,
   plausible, morally complicated organization, or like a cartoon
   villain? Did you believe the "good" cases as much as the "bad" ones?
4. **The seven witnesses**: Did each of the seven accounts feel
   distinct, or did some blur together?
5. **Pacing**: Where did you feel the urge to skim? Where did you feel
   like the plot was moving faster than you could track?
6. **Ending**: Did the selective-exposure choice feel earned, or did it
   feel like an easy way out? Did the sisters' resolution feel real?
7. **Representation**: For readers with relevant lived experience or
   expertise (domestic violence survivors/advocates, disability
   community members, immigrants from the relevant background,
   investigators/attorneys), specific, direct questions about Evelyn
   Price, Calvin Moss, Irena Petrov, and Jonah Bell's storylines
   respectively — see `00_project/risk_register.md`'s flagged items.

## Process (once applicable)

Feedback logged in `feedback_register.md`; decisions made in response
logged in `revision_decisions.md`, cross-referenced to
`DECISION_LOG.md` for anything with downstream canonical consequences.

## Prerequisite

A complete manuscript draft, ideally post-developmental-edit
(`10_revision/developmental_edit.md`).
