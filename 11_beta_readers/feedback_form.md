# Beta Reader Feedback Form

STATUS: DRAFT — template ready, not yet deployable (no manuscript).

## Template

For each of `beta_reader_guide.md`'s seven question areas: a 1–5 rating
plus a required free-text "why" (no rating without reasoning — a bare
number is not useful data). Plus:

- Which chapter did you feel most engaged in?
- Which chapter did you feel most tempted to skim?
- Which of the seven witnesses was your favorite / least memorable?
- Any point where you predicted a reveal correctly — which chapter, and
  what tipped you off? (Direct fairness-audit data —
  `05_mystery/fairness_audit.md`.)
- Any point where a reveal felt unfair or came from nowhere?
- Open feedback.

## Prerequisite

A complete manuscript draft.
