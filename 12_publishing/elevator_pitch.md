## 30-second version

THE LAST TENANT is a psychological thriller about an identity-fraud
investigator whose sister vanishes into a Brooklyn apartment building
where seven neighbors remember her as seven different people — and
whose search for the truth forces her to realize the same organization
that manufactured her sister's disappearance may have manufactured her
own childhood, decades ago, in the same building. It's a mystery about
whether a life built out of real relationships and false paperwork is
still, in some sense, real — and a story about two sisters who loved
each other badly, one demanding proof, one demanding belief, neither of
them right.

## One-line hook

Seven people remember the same missing woman as seven different people.
None of them are lying.
