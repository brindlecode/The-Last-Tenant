## Primary (source brief's own version, retained as the strongest)

After her estranged younger sister disappears, an insurance identity-
fraud investigator moves into the sister's last known apartment. Every
resident remembers the missing woman under a different name, profession,
history, and personality, while official records insist the apartment
has been vacant for twelve years.

## Alternates (for different pitch contexts)

- **Theme-forward**: A woman who has spent her career proving other
  people's lives are fake has to decide what she'll believe about her
  own — after her sister vanishes into a network that manufactures not
  just false identities, but false memories of them.
- **Hook-forward**: Seven people in the same Brooklyn building
  remember the same missing woman as seven different people. None of
  them are lying.
- **Stakes-forward**: To find her sister, an insurance fraud
  investigator has to prove that her own childhood — and possibly her
  own name — was never real to begin with.

## Usage note

The primary logline is the one used in `02_novel_bible/master_premise.md`
and should be treated as canon-consistent; the alternates are pitch
variants for different audiences (agents, retailers, readers) and should
be checked against the finished manuscript before any public use.
