# Gate 4 — Structural Outline

Status: **COMPLETE** (Part I chapter-level; Parts II–III at part-level pending
detailed outlining when drafting reaches them — this is normal sequencing,
not an open issue, see note at end of file)

Target length: ~95,000–105,000 words across three parts, roughly 32
chapters. Single POV (Nora), close third, past tense, throughout — see
`registers/voice-style-guide.md`.

## Part I — "The Tenancy" (~10 chapters, target ~30,000 words)

Arrival through the first undeniable physical proof that Cass Ferris did
not leave voluntarily. Ends with Nora holding evidence, not just unease.

1. **Move-in day.** Nora arrives at Sovereign Mill; meets Pye and Cole; the
   too-clean flat; the "FERRIS" buzzer sticker; forwarded mail. Ends: finds
   the torn notebook-page corner wedged under loose skirting.
2. **Settling in.** Unpacking; professional habits surface (fire doors,
   escape routes); meets Rena, gets the friendly-gossip version of Cass's
   departure; Marcus call establishes the marriage's unfinished business;
   finds the locked Plant Room with fresh scuff marks; ends glimpsing Cass's
   still-logged-in portal notification.
3. **The dossier thread.** Nora pulls public planning/building-control
   records; finds the fast-tracked private inspector sign-off and Cass's
   own pre-tenancy objection filed during planning; coffee with Priya
   surfaces Nora's fear she's "doing the thing again." Ends: Cole's welcome
   dinner invitation, where he names Cass unprompted.
4. **First real crack.** Nora presses Pye about Cass; his "fourth floor"
   slip. She goes up, finds Flat 10's door propped, sees the freshly
   patched section and — among genuine renovation offcuts — the rest of
   Cass's notebook. Takes it. Nearly caught. Ends holding physical proof
   the "moved out" story is false.
5. *(beat-level, to be expanded to a full Gate 5 brief when drafting
   reaches it)* Aftermath: Nora reads what the notebook safely tells her;
   tells Priya everything in full for the first time; Marcus's paperwork
   deadline forces a trip back toward her old life, raising the cost of
   what she's getting drawn into.
6. Nora reaches one of Cass's professional contacts (an editor/colleague)
   by phone; learns Cass's disappearance was out of character
   professionally, not just socially — external corroboration beyond the
   building.
7. Cole registers that Nora is digging — a small, deniable escalation
   (unannounced access to her flat "to fix something," or a comment that
   reveals he's tracking her attention). First hint of danger aimed at her
   specifically.
8. A building social event lets Nora observe Cole among the full resident
   cast and attempt another look at the fourth floor/Flat 4's absentee
   owner; a near-miss.
9. A direct one-on-one scene, Nora and Cole: he addresses her "questions"
   without confirming anything — a masterclass in his control that leaves
   her doubting her own judgment again.
10. Part I closer: Nora makes a decisive choice to pursue this formally (or
    to keep what she has rather than hand it over); parallel close of a
    piece of Marcus business gives her a clean personal stake. Ends on a
    signal — wordless, deniable — that the building, or Cole, knows she has
    the notebook.

## Part II — "The Report" (~12 chapters, target ~38,000 words) — part-level
only at this stage

Escalating investigation and escalating danger, run in parallel with the
practical and emotional work of finishing the divorce. Nora pursues the
approved-inspector thread to its source; the stairwell defect is confirmed
in professional terms; Pye's avoided knowledge starts to break down under
his own guilt; Cole's control costs him visible effort for the first time;
Marcus subplot reaches its own resolution, independent of the mystery, so
Nora enters the climax having already settled who she trusts. Ends on a
direct confrontation or overt move by Cole that removes plausible
deniability from at least one side.

## Part III — "The Last Tenant" (~10 chapters, target ~30,000 words) —
part-level only at this stage

Cass's fate is confirmed; the concealment in Flat 10 is discovered; Pye's
reckoning with what he did and didn't let himself know; Cole's downfall
engineered through the same public-record mechanism Cass began — Nora
finishes the investigation using Cass's own methods, deliberately mirrored.
Resolution addresses the title directly: who gets to be a tenant, who
decides when it's time to leave, and what Nora chooses to do with the home
she's fought for.

## Sequencing note

Per `CLAUDE.md`, Gate 5 requires a locked, detailed brief before a chapter
is drafted. Part I chapters 1–4 have full Gate 5 briefs now (see
`bible/05-chapter-briefs-part-1.md`) because they are the current drafting
target. Chapters 5–10 have the beat-level summaries above, sufficient to
lock Part I's shape and dependencies (clue payoffs, Marcus subplot timing,
Cole's escalation curve) without prematurely over-specifying scenes that
should be written closer to when they're drafted, once Chapters 1–4 have
actually been through the audit pipeline and any downstream adjustments are
known. This is intentional sequencing, not a gap — Gate 4 is complete for
its purpose (locking structure and dependencies), and each part's detailed
Gate 5 briefs are produced part-by-part as drafting reaches them.
