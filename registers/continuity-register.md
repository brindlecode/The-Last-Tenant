## Building layout quick reference (from Gate 1 — do not contradict)

  bike store, courtyard door, Pye's flat. **Courtyard** (established
  Ch.3–4, not in the original Gate 1 text but consistent with it): raised
  beds along the old loading bay that Pye tends — a *separate* amenity
  space from the roof terrace's raised beds one level up; keep both, don't
  conflate them.
- 1st: Flats 1–3; gym/laundry.
- 2nd: Flats 4–6. **Nora = Flat 6. Rena = Flat 5**, directly across the
  landing. Flat 4 owner-occupied, absentee.
- 3rd: Flats 7–9.
- 4th: Flats 10–11; the Plant Room (steel door, keyed, signed "PLANT ROOM —
  AUTHORISED ACCESS ONLY"), beside the boxed-in top of the original hoist
  shaft. **Flat 10** = the "mid-renovation" flat.
- 5th/roof: The Loft (Cole's flat); shared roof terrace. Note: Ch.1
  describes the building as "five storeys" on the exterior — read as the
  original 1868 mill structure (Ground+1st+2nd+3rd+4th); the Loft is a
  later rooftop conversion added during 2016–18, not counted among the
  original "five storeys." Not a contradiction, but keep future exterior
  descriptions consistent with this reading (i.e., don't describe the
  building as "six storeys" from outside).
- Vertical circulation: the Hoist (loud, original goods lift, lattice gate,
  serves all floors) and the original stone stair (quiet, enclosed behind a
  lightweight partition since conversion — this last technical detail is
  Nora's private professional observation at most in Ch.1–4, never
  explained on the page).
  - Naming convention: Nora doesn't yet know/use the building's own name
    for the lift in Ch.1 (calls it "the lift," "the hoist" lowercase,
    generic — correct, keep Ch.1 as-is). **Resolved in Ch.2**: a small
    brass plate on the lattice gate reads "THE HOIST"; she clocks it in
    the opening paragraph and plays along. Capitalized "the Hoist" is
    correct usage from Ch.2 onward.
