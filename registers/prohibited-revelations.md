# Prohibited Revelations Register (living, enforced per chapter)

Standing list, authoritative source `bible/03-mystery-architecture.md`.
Every chapter's audit must end with an explicit PASS/FAIL sign-off against
every item below. A single FAIL blocks that chapter from being marked
complete until revised.

## Standing prohibited list (Chapters 1–4; re-confirm at each part
boundary in later phases, since the list loosens as the novel progresses)

1. Whether Cass Ferris is alive or dead.
2. Julian Cole's causal role in Cass's death.
3. The location or existence of Cass's remains.
4. That Desmond Pye performed concealment construction work (his
   unexplained, avoided knowledge may show; no confirmation).
5. The technical specifics of the stair fire-safety defect, named as such.
6. The existence/contents of Cass's full dossier, or who she told, beyond
   the fair-play clues in the clue register.
7. The contents of the unopened Rookford Building Control Partnership
   envelope.
8. Julian Cole's financial motive/exposure (his interiority is never
   accessed at all — POV rule, not just a content rule).
