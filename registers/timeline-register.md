# Timeline Register (living)

## Secret backstory timeline (fixed reference from Gate 3 — do not alter
without a `DECISION_LOG.md` entry and a matching Gate 3 revision)

- **2016–2018**: Sovereign Mill converted by Julian Cole/Sovereign Living.
  Original stair enclosed behind lightweight partition, not rebuilt to
  full fire-rated separation. Fast-tracked private-inspector sign-off.
- **~3 years before story start**: Cass Ferris begins researching
  fast-tracked fire-safety sign-offs across Rookford conversions,
  Sovereign Mill among several examples; files a public objection during
  a later planning application at the Mill under her own name. (Corrected
  from an earlier "~2 years" planning note — Ch.3's drafted prose states
  "three years before Nora had ever heard the words Sovereign Mill," and
  `registers/character-register.md`'s Ch.3 log independently confirmed
  the same figure. Found as a cross-document discrepancy during the
  combined four-chapter audit; the register is corrected to match the two
  independently-agreeing sources rather than the other way around.)
- **January (approx. 10 months before story start)**: Cass moves into
  Flat 6, partly to research the building from inside.
- **By March**: Cass has narrowed her reporting to Sovereign Mill
  specifically; has identified the stair defect and the inspector's
  undisclosed prior relationship with Cole.
- **Thursday 14 March (~8 months before story start)**: Cole offers Cass a
  buyout in the 4th-floor corridor; she refuses; a physical restraint
  becomes a fatal fall on the original stair. Cole conceals the death that
  night and over the following week: body concealed in Flat 10 (then
  mid-renovation); Pye does unexplained "insurance remedial" building work
  in Flat 10 and in Flat 6 (a party-wall access to retrieve hidden notes,
  producing the amateur patch); Cass's phone used for ~72 hours to fake a
  "gone travelling" narrative to Rena, her landlord contact, and her
  editor, then destroyed; Flat 6 professionally deep-cleaned. Cass's
  notebook is separated from her other effects and ends up discarded among
  genuine construction offcuts in Flat 10 days later — an accident of
  housekeeping, not intent.
- **8 months of vacancy/limbo**: Flat 6 sits empty (a fact never stated
  baldly on the page — implied by the too-clean flat and forwarded mail);
  Flat 10 remains "mid-renovation" indefinitely as cover.
- **Mid-November (story start)**: Nora signs for and moves into Flat 6.

carried by weather; must stay consistent chapter to chapter)

  loading the car and hadn't let up by evening.
