# Chapter 2 Audit Report — "Settling In"

## Critique (pre-revision)

First draft came in at 1,847 words against a 2,600 target — a real
pacing gap, not a rounding issue, so this pass required expansion, not
just polish. It also surfaced three concrete problems on re-read:

1. **Underweight scene 1**: the "professional habits surface uninvited"
   beat (Gate 5's scene 1) was compressed into a single short paragraph
   before jumping straight to Rena — not enough room for the montage the
   brief asked for, and no establishing use yet of the Ropewalk Café
   (world-bible location, needed before Chapter 3 relies on it).
2. **A real timeline break**: the day-by-day montage ran Monday → Tuesday
   → Wednesday → Thursday (Rena), but the following scene opened "Marcus
   called on Wednesday" — a jump backward in the same chapter.
3. **A near-miss prohibited-revelation leak**: in drafting the portal
   scene, the message badge was described as waiting "since March" —
   which is Cass's actual (confidential, Gate 3) disappearance month.
   Nora has no in-fiction way to know that date; her only source is the
   tenancy file's "eight months," a duration, not a calendar month.
   Caught before this pass finalized, not after.

## Revision

- Expanded scene 1 into a fuller montage: added the exit-count and
  extinguisher-tag beats, plus the Ropewalk Café/Sam introduction, giving
  the "settling in" title real weight before Rena appears. Also used this
  expansion to resolve how "the Hoist" becomes a proper noun on the page
  (a brass plate on the lift gate, clocked in the opening paragraph) —
  previously only planned as "a resident says it aloud," which hadn't
  actually been written; the plate is a cleaner, single-paragraph fix.
- Fixed the timeline break by moving Marcus's call from Wednesday to
  Friday (and his deadline line from "before Friday" to "by Monday"),
  restoring forward-only chronology across the chapter.
- Fixed the near-miss: "waiting... since March" changed to "waiting...
  for months" — grounded in what Nora actually has access to.
- Added three smaller scenes/beats to close the word-count gap on merit
  rather than padding: a short Rena exchange about why she rents by
  choice (deepens her as a contrast to Nora, and independently
  corroborates Cole's hyper-attentive-landlord pattern via the radiator
  anecdote); a coda after the Marcus call that lands the Aldermoor Road
  wound concretely instead of leaving it as backstory summary; a callback
  line tying the laundry-room notice's "temporarily" to the Plant Room
  scuff marks within the same scene, tightening clue #7/#8's pairing.
- Fixed a same-chapter repetition ("the better part of a year" used twice
  within 20 lines of each other) by varying the second instance — which
  also happened to be the fix needed for the March/POV issue above, so
  both were resolved in the same edit.

Post-revision word count: 2,404 (prose only, excluding heading).
