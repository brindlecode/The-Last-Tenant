## Findings

**One consistency defect found and corrected during this verification:**
Gate 3's original draft had the Chapter 1 notebook-page fragment torn
during the same night Pye accessed the Flat 6 party wall to retrieve
Cass's hidden notes — but Gate 3 also stated Cass's actual notebook was in
her bag with her when she died, which is a contradiction (the fragment
couldn't be torn from a notebook that wasn't in the flat). Corrected: the
fragment now has a mundane, pre-death origin (an ordinary tear from months
of use, unrelated to the cover-up), and the path of the full notebook from
Cass's bag to Flat 10 is now made explicit (Cole stripped the bag in Flat
10 the night of the concealment; the notebook was swept up with genuine
renovation debris days later). This also improves the clue on its own
terms — the Chapter 1 fragment is now fair *because* its placement implies
nothing beyond "Cass sat and worked here," which is exactly the kind of
clue that should read as ambiguous in Chapter 1 and only become damning in
hindsight once Chapter 4 supplies its match. See
`bible/03-mystery-architecture.md` for the corrected text.

No other contradictions found. Specifically checked and confirmed
consistent:

- Every gate file's own "Open issues" section reads **None**.
- Character names/spellings match exactly across Gates 2–3 and
  `registers/character-register.md` and `continuity-register.md`.
- Building geography (floor-by-floor, Flat 6/Flat 5/Flat 10/Plant Room/The
  Loft) matches exactly between Gate 1 and `continuity-register.md`.
- Backstory timeline arithmetic holds: move-in ~10 months before story
  start (January) → death ~8 months before story start (14 March) is a
  ~2-month tenancy before the incident, consistent with "by March" language
  in Gate 3; 8 months of vacancy to a mid-November story start is

  Confirmed against `registers/timeline-register.md`.
- Clue numbering (1–15) matches exactly between Gate 3's clue map and
  `registers/clue-register.md`, including the red-herring table.
- Word-count arithmetic: Chapter 1–4 targets (2,600 + 2,600 + 2,700 +
  2,900) sum to the 10,800 stated as the unit total in Gate 5. Part-level
  targets in Gate 4 (30,000 + 38,000 + 30,000 = 98,000 across 10 + 12 + 10

- No named real people, brands, or real addresses used anywhere in Gates
  0–5 (Rookford, the Fenn, Marsh Wharf, Sovereign Mill, Hollis & Vane, etc.
  are confirmed invented per `bible/01-world-and-setting.md`).
- POV/knowledge-state check: nothing in Gate 5's Chapter 1–4 briefs asks
  Nora to notice, say, or conclude anything her Gate 2 knowledge-state

  an interiority beat — consistent with the close-third single-POV rule in
  `registers/voice-style-guide.md`.
- `registers/prohibited-revelations.md`'s eight-item list matches Gate 3's
  prohibited list exactly, with a per-chapter sign-off table initialized
